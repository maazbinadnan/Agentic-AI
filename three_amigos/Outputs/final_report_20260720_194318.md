# Requirements Engineering Report

## 1. Executive Summary

**Project Name:** LiveFootball Mobile App  
**Domain:** Sports Media / Mobile Applications

**Project Description:**  
LiveFootball is a cross-platform mobile application (Android and iOS) designed to provide football fans with a central, personalized platform for real-time news, live results, team/player information, and live stream links. The app emphasizes high performance (startup within 2 seconds), reliability under high load (100,000 concurrent users), offline usability, legal compliance (GDPR, transmission rights), and a modular, accessible user interface.

**Key Stakeholder Groups and User Roles:**
- End Users (Football Fans)
- Administrators (Content/Compliance Managers)
- System/Technical Stakeholders (Developers, QA, DevOps)

**Summary Statistics:**
- **Total User Needs Identified:** 29
- **Total Functional Requirements (FRs):** 21
- **Total Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs):** 10
- **Total User Stories:** 20
- **MoSCoW Priority Breakdown:**
  - Must Have: 25
  - Should Have: 4
  - Could Have: 0
  - Won't Have: 0

- "Ready for interaction" at startup is now defined as all primary navigation elements rendered and tappable within 2 seconds; content modules may load asynchronously.

**Overall Confidence Assessment:**

## 2. User Research Summary

### 2.1 Research Methodology

- **Methods Used:** Stakeholder interviews, analysis of competitor apps, review of user feedback from existing sports apps, and requirements workshops.
- **Participant Demographics/User Segments:**
  - Football fans (ages 16–65, global, with a focus on Europe)
  - Power users (follow multiple teams/leagues)
  - Casual users (occasional match viewers)
  - Administrators (content/compliance managers)
  - Technical stakeholders (developers, QA, DevOps)

### 2.2 Major Themes & Findings

#### Performance & Reliability
- **Pain Point:** "Many apps are slow to start, especially on match days."
- **Finding:** Users expect the app to be ready for use within 2 seconds and to remain responsive even during high-traffic events.
- **Constraint:** Must support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance loss.
- **Quote:** "If it takes more than a couple of seconds to load, I just use Google instead."

#### Personalization & Content Selection
- **Pain Point:** "I want to see news and results for my favorite teams, not just generic headlines."
- **Finding:** Users want to select favorite teams and preferred sports channels.
- **Quote:** "Let me pick my teams and get only their news."

#### Live Data & Streaming
- **Pain Point:** "Live scores and streams are often delayed or unavailable."
- **Finding:** Real-time live ticker and live stream links are essential; must comply with transmission rights.
- **Constraint:** Only show streams where rights are valid in the user's country.
- **Quote:** "I want to watch the match live, not just see the score."

#### User Interface & Usability
- **Pain Point:** "Too many taps to get to what I want."
- **Finding:** Users want direct, efficient access to news, live ticker, and team info.
- **Constraint:** UI must be modular, accessible, and allow for personalization.
- **Quote:** "I should be able to get to the live ticker in two taps."

#### Notifications
- **Pain Point:** "I get too many irrelevant notifications."
- **Finding:** Users want granular control over notifications (news, results, events, per team).
- **Quote:** "Let me choose what I get notified about."

#### Offline & Low Connectivity
- **Pain Point:** "App is useless when I lose signal."
- **Finding:** App must work offline with cached data and degrade gracefully.
- **Quote:** "I want to check scores even on the subway."

#### Compliance & Data Protection
- **Pain Point:** "I worry about my data privacy."
- **Finding:** GDPR compliance is mandatory; users want control over their data.
- **Constraint:** All personal data must be processed per GDPR.
- **Quote:** "I want to know how my data is used and be able to delete it."

#### Content Management & Consistency
- **Pain Point:** "Features break after updates."
- **Finding:** UI and modules must remain consistent after server updates.
- **Quote:** "Don't break my app after an update."

### US-001: Fast App Startup

**Source Requirements:** FR-001, NFR-001  
**Priority:** Must Have

**User Story:**  
> As a mobile app user, I want the app to load and be ready for interaction within 2 seconds on supported devices, so that I can quickly access football news and features, improving my satisfaction and likelihood to continue using the app.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**AC-001.1: App Starts Within 2 Seconds (Happy Path)**
```gherkin
Given the user launches the app on a supported device (see device/OS matrix covering 80% of active user base)
When the app is started from a cold state under normal network conditions
Then Home, News, Live Ticker, and Team Info navigation elements are rendered, tappable, and visually responsive within 2 seconds; tapping each element provides visual feedback within 100ms; news/live ticker modules display loading indicators if content is not yet available
And startup time is equal to or faster than top 3 competitor apps on equivalent devices (benchmarks documented and updated quarterly)
And "Ready for interaction" means all primary navigation elements are rendered and respond to tap events with visual feedback within 2 seconds; content modules may load asynchronously
```

**AC-001.2: Startup Exceeds 2 Seconds (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given the app startup exceeds 2 seconds for 3 consecutive launches
When the user launches the app
Then the app prompts the user for explicit consent to send diagnostic data (device model, OS version, app version, anonymized performance logs)
And logs the performance issue centrally
And displays a user-friendly, non-blocking loading indicator until ready
And allows the user to cancel
And diagnostic data payload, consent storage, and offline log transmission retry policy are documented
```

**AC-001.3: Unsupported Device (Boundary Condition)**
```gherkin
Given a device below minimum hardware requirements (documented and versioned)
When the user launches the app
Then the app displays a message indicating unsupported device and does not attempt full load
```

**AC-001.4: Business Impact Tracking (Business Rule)**
```gherkin
Given startup exceeds 2 seconds for >5% of users in a given week
When this threshold is reached
Then trigger a business review and user satisfaction survey; track impact on retention and NPS
```

### US-002: Offline Usability

**Source Requirements:** FR-002, NFR-003  
**Priority:** Must Have

**User Story:**  
> As a football fan, I want to access previously loaded news, team, and match data when offline, so that I can stay informed even without an internet connection.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**AC-002.1: Access Cached Content Offline (Happy Path)**
```gherkin
Given the user has previously loaded news, images (compressed to max 200KB each), and basic stats for favorite teams
When the device is offline
Then the app displays cached text, images, and stats for favorite teams
And disables live features with a tooltip: "Live features require an internet connection."
```

**AC-002.2: Manual Refresh and Cache Management (Happy Path)**
```gherkin
Given the user is online
When they manually trigger a refresh
Then cached content is updated
```

**AC-002.3: Cache Size and Eviction Policy (Boundary Condition)**
```gherkin
Given the cache exceeds 100 MB (default, configurable in settings: 50–500MB, 1–30 days)
When new content is cached
Then least recently used data is evicted first
And favorite teams’ news and match data are always retained if possible; if favorite teams’ data exceeds 100MB, retain most recent 7 days of news/match data per team, up to a max of 20MB per team; evict oldest items first
```

**AC-002.4: Cache Corruption (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given the cache is corrupted after an app update (detected via checksum/hash validation on startup)
When the user attempts to access offline content
Then the app clears the cache and prompts the user with an in-app dialog explaining cache corruption and offers a "Reload Data" button; if offline, retries notification on next online event
```

**AC-002.5: No Cached Data Available (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given the user has never loaded any content
When the device is offline
Then the app displays a message: "No offline content available."
```

**AC-002.6: Minimum Offline Content (Business Rule)**
```gherkin
Given offline caching is enabled
When content is prioritized
Then cache must include text, images, and match data for favorite teams for the last 7 days
And cache size and retention period are user-configurable in settings
```

**AC-002.7: User Notification on Cache Clear (Business Rule)**
```gherkin
Given cache is cleared due to corruption
When this occurs
Then notify the user with a clear explanation and offer a link to reload data when online
```

### US-003: High Concurrent User Support

**Source Requirements:** FR-003, NFR-004  
**Priority:** Must Have

**User Story:**  
> As a football fan, I want the app to remain responsive and functional during high-traffic periods, so that I can reliably access information even when many users are online.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**AC-003.1: App Performance Under Load (Happy Path)**
```gherkin
Given backend APIs support 100,000 concurrent users with <500 ms average response time for live ticker and notifications (test conditions documented: simulated load, geographic distribution, network latency)
When a user interacts with the app
Then the app maintains a response time within 5% of normal performance for live ticker and notifications; news and other features may degrade gracefully (disable live ticker and notifications, display "Service temporarily degraded" banner, log event)
And backend and third-party APIs expose health endpoints and real-time metrics; app polls these endpoints to adjust UI and log degradation events
```

**AC-003.2: Backend Overload (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given backend is overloaded or rate-limited (HTTP 429/503 or latency > 2 seconds)
When a user interacts with the app
Then the app displays a user-friendly message: "Service temporarily degraded"
And logs the event for business review
```

**AC-003.3: Third-Party API Degradation (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given third-party API performance degrades
When the app detects failure
Then the app displays a message and logs the issue for business review
And dependencies on third-party APIs and their business impact are documented
```

**AC-003.4: Auto-Scaling and Cost Threshold (Business Rule)**
```gherkin
Given response time degrades by more than 5%
When auto-scaling is triggered and exceeds monthly infrastructure budget
Then the product owner is notified via email/SMS within 1 hour for approval
And system triggers an alert for investigation
And auto-scaling events and cost overruns are logged to central monitoring
```

**AC-003.5: Business Impact Tracking (Business Rule)**
```gherkin
Given performance degradation leads to SLA breach or >2% user drop-off during high-traffic events
When this occurs
Then escalate to business review; track impact on retention and NPS
```

### US-008: Live Ticker for Favorite Teams

**Source Requirements:** FR-008, FR-020, FR-021  
**Priority:** Must Have

**User Story:**  
> As a football fan, I want to see a live ticker with real-time results for my favorite teams, so that I can stay updated on matches as they happen.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

**AC-008.1: Live Ticker Updates in Real Time (Happy Path)**
```gherkin
Given a favorite team is playing and the user has opted in for live ticker notifications
When a new event (goal, card, substitution, injury) occurs
Then the live ticker updates in the UI within 5 seconds and a notification is sent
And live ticker covers all top-tier leagues; coverage for lower-tier leagues is documented and versioned
And polling interval is configurable (default 10s, min 5s) and validated against backend rate limits and battery usage
```

**AC-008.2: No Internet Connection (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given the device is offline
When the user views the live ticker

```

**AC-008.3: API Failure or Latency (Negative/Edge Case)**
```gherkin
Given API latency exceeds 5 seconds or external server is unreachable
When the app attempts to retrieve live data
Then the app displays "Live updates delayed" and shows the most recent data
And falls back to polling every 10 seconds if push fails
```

**AC-008.4: Real-Time Delivery (Technical Rule)**
```gherkin
Given live ticker events occur
When updates are delivered
Then real-time updates are delivered via push notifications or WebSocket; fallback to polling every 10 seconds if push fails
```

**AC-008.5: Notification Opt-in/out (Business Rule)**

## 6. Three Amigos Review Summary

### 6.1 Review Process Overview

- **Number of Review Rounds Conducted:** 3
- **Participants in Each Round:** Product Owner (PO), Developer (Dev), QA Engineer (QA)
- **Overall Review Methodology:** Iterative review of user stories and acceptance criteria, focusing on business value, technical feasibility, testability, and traceability. Each round addressed ambiguities, added measurable criteria, and ensured compliance and negative scenarios were covered.

### 6.2 Key Discussion Points

#### Product Owner Feedback
- Emphasized the need for clear business value and market benchmarks (e.g., startup time compared to competitors).
- Requested explicit mapping of ambiguous terms (e.g., "ready for interaction," "quickly and smoothly") to measurable, testable criteria.
- Highlighted the importance of compliance (GDPR, transmission rights) and user satisfaction metrics (retention, NPS).
- Challenged stories lacking business rules or competitive context.

#### Developer Feedback
- Raised concerns about technical feasibility of 2-second startup across all devices; requested device/OS matrix and minimum hardware requirements.
- Sought clarification on what constitutes "fully loaded" and "ready for interaction."
- Flagged edge cases (unsupported devices, backend/API failures, auto-scaling/cost overruns).
- Requested documentation of API contracts, polling intervals, and fallback strategies.

#### QA Engineer Feedback
- Stressed the need for unambiguous, testable acceptance criteria (e.g., which UI elements must be interactive within 2 seconds).
- Requested explicit negative and boundary test cases (e.g., cache corruption, registration retry limits).
- Highlighted regression risks and the need for versioned documentation of flows and test environments.
- Ensured all compliance and accessibility requirements were testable.
