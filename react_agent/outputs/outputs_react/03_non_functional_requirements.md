# Non-Functional Requirements

## NFR1: Performance
- The app shall load and be ready for use within 2 seconds of launch.
- The app shall provide real-time updates for live scores and notifications with a maximum delay of 2 seconds.

## NFR2: Reliability
- The app shall remain operational and provide access to previously loaded data during temporary network outages.
- The app shall maintain consistent functionality and user interface across server and app updates.

## NFR3: Scalability
- The app shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance degradation, especially during peak match days.

## NFR4: Usability
- The app shall provide an intuitive and easy-to-navigate user interface.
- Registration and login processes shall be simple and quick, requiring no more than 2 minutes to complete.

## NFR5: Compatibility
- The app shall be compatible with the latest two major versions of Android and iOS.

## NFR6: Security & Privacy
- All personal data shall be processed in compliance with GDPR.
- The app shall ensure secure transmission and storage of user data.

## NFR7: Availability
- The app shall be available for download and use 99.9% of the time, excluding scheduled maintenance.

## NFR8: Maintainability
- The app shall be designed to allow for easy updates and integration of new features without disrupting existing functionality.

## NFR9: Localization
- The app shall comply with local streaming rights and only display live streams where permitted.

## NFR10: Support & Feedback
- The app shall provide mechanisms for users to submit feedback and report issues easily.
