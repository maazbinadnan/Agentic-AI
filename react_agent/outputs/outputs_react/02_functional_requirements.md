# Functional Requirements

## FR1: Platform Support
- The app shall be available for both Android and iOS devices.

## FR2: Fast Startup
- The app shall fully load and be ready to use within 2 seconds of launch.

## FR3: News Display
- The app shall allow users to view current football news for their selected favorite teams.
- The app shall allow users to select and follow preferred sports channels.

## FR4: Live Ticker
- The app shall provide a live ticker module to display real-time match results for favorite teams.
- The live ticker shall retrieve live data via an API from an external server.

## FR5: League and Competition Coverage
- The app shall provide coverage for all national and international football leagues and competitions.

## FR6: Team and Player Information
- The app shall display detailed information about teams and players at any time.

## FR7: Live Stream Integration
- The app shall provide links to live streams from providers (e.g., DAZN, Sky Sport) when broadcasts are available and rights are fulfilled in the user's country.

## FR8: Notification System
- The app shall notify users about current events when notifications are enabled.

## FR9: User Registration and Login
- The app shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup.

## FR10: Personalization
- The app shall allow users to personalize their experience by following favorite clubs and configuring notifications.

## FR11: Social Sharing
- The app shall allow users to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.

## FR12: Offline Functionality
- The app shall remain usable offline, providing access to previously loaded data if the network connection is lost.

## FR13: High Concurrent User Support
- The app shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance degradation.

## FR14: Testing and Support
- The app shall undergo thorough testing and support processes before release to detect and resolve malfunctions.

## FR15: User Feedback
- The app shall collect and evaluate user feedback and app reviews to improve functionality.

## FR16: Data Protection
- The app shall process all personal data in compliance with GDPR.
