# User Stories and BDD Scenarios

## User Story 1: Fast App Launch
**As** a football fan,
**I want** the app to load within 2 seconds,
**so that** I can access information quickly.

**Scenario:**
Given I have installed the app,
When I launch the app,
Then it should be fully loaded and ready to use within 2 seconds.

---

## User Story 2: Personalized News Feed
**As** a user,
**I want** to see news about my favorite teams and channels,
**so that** I stay informed about what matters to me.

**Scenario:**
Given I have selected my favorite teams and channels,
When I open the news section,
Then I should see news relevant to my selections.

---

## User Story 3: Live Ticker Updates
**As** a fan,
**I want** to follow live match results for my favorite teams,
**so that** I can stay updated in real time.

**Scenario:**
Given a match is in progress,
When I view the live ticker,
Then I should see real-time updates for my selected teams.

---

## User Story 4: Live Stream Access
**As** a user,
**I want** to access live stream links when available and permitted,
**so that** I can watch matches directly from the app.

**Scenario:**
Given a live broadcast is available and rights are fulfilled in my country,
When I access the match page,
Then I should see a link to the live stream.

---

## User Story 5: Notifications
**As** a user,
**I want** to receive notifications about news and results for my favorite teams,
**so that** I never miss important updates.

**Scenario:**
Given I have enabled notifications,
When a relevant event occurs,
Then I should receive a notification promptly.

---

## User Story 6: Social Sharing
**As** a social sharer,
**I want** to share news and reports via social media,
**so that** I can inform my friends and followers.

**Scenario:**
Given I am viewing a news article or match report,
When I tap the share button,
Then I should be able to share the content via my chosen social platform.

---

## User Story 7: Offline Access
**As** a user,
**I want** the app to remain usable offline,
**so that** I can access previously loaded information without a network connection.

**Scenario:**
Given I have previously loaded data,
When I lose network connectivity,
Then I should still be able to view the cached information.

---

## User Story 8: Easy Registration and Login
**As** a new user,
**I want** to register and log in quickly,
**so that** I can start using the app without hassle.

**Scenario:**
Given I have downloaded the app,
When I start the app for the first time,
Then I should be able to register and log in within 2 minutes.

---

## User Story 9: Data Privacy
**As** a privacy-conscious user,
**I want** my personal data to be handled securely and in compliance with GDPR,
**so that** I can trust the app with my information.

**Scenario:**
Given I provide personal data,
When the app processes or stores my data,
Then it should comply with GDPR requirements.

---

## User Story 10: High Load Reliability
**As** a user,
**I want** the app to work reliably even during peak times,
**so that** I can access features without slowdowns or crashes.

**Scenario:**
Given there are up to 100,000 concurrent users,
When I use the app during a major match,
Then the app should remain responsive and functional.
