# User Needs and Personas

## Personas

### 1. Football Fanatic
- Age: 16-50
- Tech-savvy, follows multiple teams and leagues
- Wants instant access to live scores, news, and streams

### 2. Casual Follower
- Age: 18-65
- Interested in major events, follows favorite team(s)
- Wants easy navigation, notifications, and occasional live updates

### 3. Social Sharer
- Age: 16-40
- Enjoys sharing news and match reports on social media
- Wants seamless sharing features

### 4. International User
- Age: 18-60
- Follows teams and leagues from different countries
- Needs multi-league coverage and compliance with local streaming rights

## High-Level User Needs
- Fast and smooth app experience (load within 2 seconds)
- Access to real-time live scores and match data
- Personalized news and notifications for favorite teams
- Ability to select and follow preferred sports channels
- Detailed team and player information
- Access to live stream links (with rights compliance)
- Reliable operation under poor network conditions and offline
- Easy registration and login
- Social media sharing of news and reports
- Data privacy and GDPR compliance
- Consistent user interface and experience across updates
- Support for high concurrent user loads (up to 100,000)
