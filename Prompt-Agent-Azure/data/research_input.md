Interview Notes: Subject A (First-Year Undergraduate)

"When I went to the freshers' fair, it was completely overwhelming. There are over 150 societies. I'm really into niche things, like mathematical modeling in sports and board games, but I couldn't easily find groups for that. I wish there was a way the university app could just look at my profile, maybe my course or the tags I select, and recommend 3 or 4 societies I'd actually like. Right now, I just rely on word of mouth."

Interview Notes: Subject B (Postgraduate / Master's Student)

"My schedule is incredibly tight between classes and my part-time job as a community assistant. I want to be involved in tech societies, but I don't have time to attend general meetings. I only want to be notified about specific, high-value events—like a guest lecture on machine learning or a weekend winter retreat—and only if they don't clash with my evening shifts. If an event is full, I want to be put on a waitlist automatically."

Survey Feedback Summary: Society Executives (n=12)

Pain Point: 85% of society execs find it difficult to reach the right audience for their events.
Feature Request: "We want a dashboard where we can create an event, tag it with keywords (e.g., 'AI', 'Networking', 'Python'), and have the system automatically push notifications to students who have those interests saved in their profiles. Also, we need the system to stop accepting RSVPs once the room capacity is hit."
