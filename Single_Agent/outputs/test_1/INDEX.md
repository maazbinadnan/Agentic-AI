# INDEX: LiveFootball App Requirements & Deliverables

## Executive Summary

This package contains the complete business analysis and interaction design deliverables for the LiveFootball mobile app, targeting Android and iOS. The app provides football fans with real-time scores, news, live streams, detailed team/player info, notifications, personalization, and social sharing, with a focus on speed, reliability, scalability, offline capability, and GDPR compliance.
