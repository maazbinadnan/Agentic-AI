# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access live scores and results of my favorite football teams in real time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During match days or when following ongoing games.

### UN-002: I need to view current football news and updates about my favorite teams and leagues.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Daily app usage, pre/post-match, or during breaking news.

### UN-003: I need to watch live streams of football matches when available and permitted.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When a live broadcast is ongoing and rights are fulfilled.

### UN-004: I need to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During onboarding, registration, or in settings.

### UN-005: I need to receive notifications about news, scores, and events related to my favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When important events occur or as configured by the user.

### UN-006: I need the app to load quickly and run smoothly, even during high traffic.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Every app launch, especially on match days.

### UN-007: I need the app to function reliably even with limited network coverage or offline.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When traveling, in stadiums, or during network interruptions.

### UN-008: I need to access detailed information about teams and players at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Research, pre/post-match, or general browsing.

### UN-009: I need to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After reading news or match reports.

### UN-010: I need assurance that my personal data is protected and processed in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During registration, login, and ongoing app usage.

### UN-011: I need to register and log in easily to access personalized features.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** At app startup or when accessing personalized content.

### UN-012: I need the app to be thoroughly tested and supported to avoid malfunctions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout app lifecycle, especially after updates.
