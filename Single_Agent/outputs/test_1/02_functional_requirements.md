# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that displays real-time scores and results for selected football teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-005
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to view current football news and updates for their favorite teams and leagues.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-004
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for football matches, displaying them only when rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to personalize their experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a notification system that informs users about news, scores, and events related to their favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to access detailed information about teams and players at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Should Have

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Should Have

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide registration and login functionality that is simple and can be completed at app startup.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain a consistent user interface that connects and manages all modules.
- **Source Need:** UN-004, UN-007
- **Priority:** Must Have

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall evaluate user feedback and app reviews to improve functionality.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Should Have
