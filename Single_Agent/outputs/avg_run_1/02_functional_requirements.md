# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide user registration and login capabilities at app startup so users can access personalized features with minimal steps.
- **Source Need:** UN-007, UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select, update, and persist favorite clubs, preferred sports channels, and notification preferences within their profile or settings.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall display a personalized football news feed based on the user’s selected favorite teams and preferred sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve and display live scores and live ticker updates for favorite teams and selected matches using data from an external API.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide browse and detail views for national and international leagues, competitions, teams, and players.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall display official live stream links for matches only after the live broadcast has begun and only when transmission rights are valid for the user’s country.
- **Source Need:** UN-005, UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall send notifications for configured news and match events related to the user’s favorite teams when the user has activated notifications.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to share news articles and match reports to external social media channels directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide an offline-capable experience by allowing users to access cached content and the last successfully retrieved data when network connectivity is limited or unavailable.
- **Source Need:** UN-009, UN-001
- **Priority:** High
