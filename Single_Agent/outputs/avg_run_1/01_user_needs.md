# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access the app and core information quickly so I can start using it immediately.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When launching the app before or during a match, users expect the platform to open quickly and present key content without delay.

### UN-002: I need to personalize the app around my favorite clubs and preferred channels so I receive relevant football content.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After onboarding or within settings, users want to tailor the experience to teams and sources they care about most.

### UN-003: I need to follow live results and match events in real time so I can stay updated during matches.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While matches are being played, users want immediate visibility of scores and live ticker updates.

### UN-004: I need comprehensive coverage of leagues, competitions, teams, and players so I can explore football information beyond my favorites.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When browsing the app outside live match moments, users want broad domestic and international football coverage.

### UN-005: I need to find available live stream links lawfully in my region so I can quickly access official broadcasts.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Shortly before or during a live match, users want to see whether an official stream is available to them.

### UN-006: I need timely notifications about news and results for my favorite teams so I do not miss important events.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After enabling alerts, users expect the app to proactively inform them about relevant football developments.

### UN-007: I need registration and login to be simple so I can start using personalized features with minimal effort.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** New and Returning Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** At first launch or return visits, users want fast access to the app and their saved preferences.

### UN-008: I need to share news and match information easily so I can discuss football updates with others.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Socially Active Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When users discover an interesting article or match result, they want to distribute it directly via social platforms.

### UN-009: I need the app to remain usable under poor connectivity or offline so I can still access important content reliably.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While traveling, in stadiums, or in weak network areas, users still expect access to key app functions and cached information.

### UN-010: I need confidence that my personal data is handled securely and lawfully so I can trust the platform.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During account creation, preference setup, and ongoing app use, users expect privacy and GDPR-compliant data handling.
