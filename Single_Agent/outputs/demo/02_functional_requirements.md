# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide users with app startup access to registration and login so they can create an account or authenticate before using personalized features.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow authenticated users to select, update, and store favorite teams and preferred sports channels as personalization preferences.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news aggregated for the user, including news related to selected favorite teams and preferred channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-003, UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve and display live match scores and event updates for favorite teams through an integrated live ticker module connected to an external live data API.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide browsable and searchable coverage of leagues, competitions, teams, and players, including detailed team and player information.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links for matches only after the broadcast has begun and only when transmission rights are valid for the user’s country.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to enable, disable, and manage notifications for relevant news and live results related to their favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-007, UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and match reports directly from the app through supported social media sharing mechanisms.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a usable interface during limited or absent connectivity by displaying available cached content and connection status where live retrieval is temporarily unavailable.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall process personal data required for account, personalization, and notification functions in a manner consistent with GDPR obligations.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High
