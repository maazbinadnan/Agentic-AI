# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access current football news and live results for my favorite teams so that I can stay informed at all times.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app, selects favorite teams, and views news and live results on the dashboard.

### UN-002: I need to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User configures preferences during onboarding or in settings to tailor content.

### UN-003: I need to receive real-time notifications about news and results for my favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications and receives push alerts for relevant events.

### UN-004: I need to access live stream links for matches when available and permitted in my country.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User browses match details and clicks on live stream links when rights are available.

### UN-005: I need to view detailed information about teams and players.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User navigates to team or player profiles for in-depth statistics and info.

### UN-006: I need to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads an article and uses the share function to post on social media.

### UN-007: I need the app to load quickly and run smoothly, even with high user traffic or poor network conditions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User launches the app and expects it to be ready for use within 2 seconds, regardless of network or load.

### UN-008: I need the app to function reliably and remain usable even if the network connection is temporarily lost.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User continues to use the app during intermittent connectivity, with cached data available.

### UN-009: I need my personal data to be protected and processed in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers, logs in, and manages data with assurance of privacy and legal compliance.

### UN-010: I need to register and log in quickly and easily at app startup.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads the app, registers or logs in with minimal steps, and accesses main features.

### UN-011: I need the app to be available on both Android and iOS devices.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads and installs the app from the respective app store.

### UN-012: I need the app to be thoroughly tested and supported to avoid malfunctions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User expects a stable app experience due to robust QA and support processes.

### UN-013: I need the app to continuously improve based on user feedback and reviews.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Low
- **User Journey Context:** User submits feedback or reviews and expects future updates to address issues.
