# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide registration and login capability directly at app startup so that users can create an account or authenticate with minimal steps.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow authenticated users to select, update, and store favorite clubs, preferred sports channels, and notification preferences within their profile settings.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-007, UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall display a personalized football news feed containing current news for the user’s favorite teams and content filtered by selected sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide live scores and a continuously updating live ticker for matches involving favorite teams using data retrieved from an external live-data API.
- **Source Need:** UN-004, UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide browsable coverage of national and international leagues and competitions, including access to related matches, teams, and standings or summary context where available.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall display detailed information pages for teams and players that users can access from relevant news items, matches, or competition views.
- **Source Need:** UN-005, UN-003
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links for eligible live broadcasts only after the broadcast has begun and only when transmission rights are valid for the user’s country.
- **Source Need:** UN-006, UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to activate notifications and receive alerts about relevant news, live events, and results related to their favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news items and match reports to supported social media channels using the device’s native sharing capabilities or configured integrations.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline-accessible and degraded-mode behavior by showing cached content, preserving core navigation, and synchronizing updated data when connectivity is restored.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High
