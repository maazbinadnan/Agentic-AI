# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access the app quickly and sign in or register with minimal effort.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When opening the app for the first time or returning to it, users need immediate access without a complex onboarding barrier.

### UN-002: I need to personalize the app around my favorite clubs and preferred sports channels.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After entering the app, users want to configure content preferences so the app reflects their football interests.

### UN-003: I need a central place to consume relevant football news and competition coverage.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During day-to-day use, users want to stay informed about favorite teams and broader national and international football developments.

### UN-004: I need to follow live match results and events for my favorite teams in real time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** On match days, users want live access to changing scores and match events without leaving the app.

### UN-005: I need to view detailed team and player information whenever I want deeper context.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** While reading news or following matches, users may want to drill into teams, squads, and player details.

### UN-006: I need to discover available live stream links for matches when broadcasts are legally available in my country.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a match is live, users want direct access to viewing options without being shown unavailable or unauthorized streams.

### UN-007: I need to receive timely notifications about important news and results for teams I follow.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want the app to proactively inform them of relevant developments even when they are not actively browsing.

### UN-008: I need to share football news and reports easily through social media.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Socially Active Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After finding interesting content, users want to distribute it to friends or followers without friction.

### UN-009: I need the app to remain usable and trustworthy under poor connectivity and high-demand conditions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users may access the app on the move, in stadiums, or during peak traffic, and still expect reliable service.

### UN-010: I need confidence that my personal data is handled securely and in compliance with privacy regulations.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During registration, personalization, and ongoing usage, users need assurance that their personal data is processed responsibly.
