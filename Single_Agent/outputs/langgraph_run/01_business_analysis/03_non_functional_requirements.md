# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall fully load and be ready to use within two seconds after app startup.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably with limited network coverage and provide offline access to previously loaded data.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal data in compliance with GDPR requirements.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance degradation.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available for both Android and iOS platforms.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes to detect and resolve malfunctions before release.
- **Source:** UN-013
- **Priority:** Medium
