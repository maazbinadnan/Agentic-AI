# 4. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

### US-001
**User Story:** As a user, I want to learn about CAZ schemes so that I understand their purpose and benefits.
- **Source:** FR-001
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Viewing CAZ Information**
  - **Given:** I am on the home page
  - **When:** I view the CAZ information section
  - **Then:** I see clear explanations about CAZ, its purpose, and benefits

### US-002
**User Story:** As a user, I want to view an interactive map of CAZ areas and activation times so that I can plan my journey.
- **Source:** FR-002
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Viewing CAZ Map**
  - **Given:** I am on the CAZ map page
  - **When:** I select a city or CAZ
  - **Then:** I see affected areas and activation times

### US-003
**User Story:** As a user, I want to check if my vehicle is compliant so that I know if I need to pay a charge.
- **Source:** FR-003
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Checking Vehicle Compliance**
  - **Given:** I am on the compliance check page
  - **When:** I enter my vehicle details
  - **Then:** I am shown whether my vehicle is compliant and if a charge applies

### US-004
**User Story:** As a user, I want the system to prevent me from paying if I am exempt so that I avoid unnecessary payments.
- **Source:** FR-004
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Attempting Payment for Exempt Vehicle**
  - **Given:** I have entered my vehicle details
  - **When:** I try to pay and my vehicle is exempt
  - **Then:** The system prevents payment and informs me of the exemption

### US-005
**User Story:** As a user, I want to make CAZ payments quickly and easily so that I minimize admin time.
- **Source:** FR-005
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Making a Payment**
  - **Given:** I am on the payment page
  - **When:** I enter required details and confirm payment
  - **Then:** The payment is processed and I receive confirmation

### US-006
**User Story:** As a user, I want to check if I am eligible for exemptions and apply if possible so that I avoid charges.
- **Source:** FR-006
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Checking and Applying for Exemption**
  - **Given:** I am on the exemption page
  - **When:** I enter my details
  - **Then:** I am informed of eligibility and can submit an application

### US-007
**User Story:** As a user, I want to understand differences between CAZs so that I know what to expect when traveling.
- **Source:** FR-007
- **Priority:** Medium
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Viewing CAZ Differences**
  - **Given:** I am on the CAZ comparison page
  - **When:** I select two or more CAZs
  - **Then:** I see a comparison of their rules and charges

### US-008
**User Story:** As a user, I want to access technical support if I am struggling so that I can complete my journey.
- **Source:** FR-008
- **Priority:** Medium
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Accessing Support**
  - **Given:** I am on any page
  - **When:** I click the support/help button
  - **Then:** I am shown FAQs and contact options

### US-009
**User Story:** As a user, I want to find alternatives to paying a CAZ charge so that I can avoid charges.
- **Source:** FR-009
- **Priority:** Medium
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Viewing Alternatives**
  - **Given:** I am on the alternatives page
  - **When:** I view the options
  - **Then:** I see alternative routes or transport options

### US-010
**User Story:** As a user, I want to see clear visuals of CAZ road signs and boundaries so that I know when I am entering/exiting a CAZ.
- **Source:** FR-010
- **Priority:** High
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Viewing Signposting Information**
  - **Given:** I am on the signposting page
  - **When:** I view the visuals
  - **Then:** I see images and explanations of CAZ signs and boundaries

### US-011
**User Story:** As a user, I want to apply for a refund if I paid unnecessarily so that I can get my money back.
- **Source:** FR-011
- **Priority:** Medium
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Applying for Refund**
  - **Given:** I am on the refund page
  - **When:** I enter my payment details and reason
  - **Then:** I can submit a refund request and receive confirmation

### US-012
**User Story:** As a user, I want to know what to do if I receive a PCN so that I can act accordingly.
- **Source:** FR-012
- **Priority:** Medium
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Scenario: Managing PCN**
  - **Given:** I am on the PCN information page
  - **When:** I enter my PCN details
  - **Then:** I am shown guidance on next steps
