# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Breathe better quality air in a fair and equitable way
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want improved air quality with minimal negative impact on their routine and livelihood.

### UN-002: Understand what a CAZ is and how it impacts me
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users need to understand CAZ schemes to prepare themselves or their companies.

### UN-003: Easily understand areas affected by the scheme and starting times for each CAZ
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users need to know which areas are affected and when, to plan their journeys.

### UN-004: Check if my vehicle will incur charges due to emissions
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to travel with confidence, knowing if they need to pay a charge.

### UN-005: Prevent unnecessary payments if not required
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to avoid accidental payments and the need for refunds.

### UN-006: Make easy and quick CAZ payments
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to minimize admin time when paying charges.

### UN-007: Find out if I am exempt and apply for exemption
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to avoid charges by checking/applying for exemptions.

### UN-008: Understand differences between CAZs and their charging methods
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to know what to expect when traveling in different CAZs.

### UN-009: Access technical support if struggling
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users need help to complete their journey or resolve issues.

### UN-010: Find alternatives to paying a CAZ charge
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to avoid charges by finding alternative routes or transport.

### UN-011: Clear signs on roads to show CAZ boundaries
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users need clear signposting to know when they are entering/exiting a CAZ.

### UN-012: Know how to apply for a refund
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to recover money if they paid unnecessarily.

### UN-013: Know what to do if I receive a PCN
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to act appropriately if they receive a penalty charge notice.
