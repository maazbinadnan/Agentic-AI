# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide clear information about CAZ schemes, their purpose, and benefits.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display an interactive map showing areas affected by each CAZ and their activation times.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to check vehicle compliance by entering vehicle details.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall prevent users from making payments if their vehicle is exempt or does not require a charge.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to make CAZ payments quickly and easily.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to check if they are eligible for exemptions and submit exemption applications.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide information on differences between CAZs and their charging methods.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to technical support and FAQs.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide information and guidance on alternatives to paying a CAZ charge.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall display visual representations of CAZ road signs and boundaries.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to apply for refunds for unnecessary payments.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide guidance on what to do if a user receives a PCN.
- **Source Need:** UN-013
- **Priority:** Medium
