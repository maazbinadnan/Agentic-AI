# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall be accessible and usable on both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Source:** UN-001, UN-002, UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall comply with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
- **Source:** UN-001, UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall ensure all user data is processed and stored in compliance with GDPR.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a response time of less than 2 seconds for all core user actions under normal load.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall support at least 10,000 concurrent users without degradation of service.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available 99.9% of the time, excluding scheduled maintenance.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline access to CAZ information and maps.
- **Source:** UN-002, UN-003
- **Priority:** Medium
