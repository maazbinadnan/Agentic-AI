# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need the app to open quickly and be ready to use immediately.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A user launches the app before or during a match and expects immediate access to live information without delay.

### UN-002: I need a simple way to register, log in, and access my account at startup.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A new or returning user opens the app and needs uncomplicated authentication before personalizing the experience.

### UN-003: I need to personalize the app around my favorite clubs and preferred sports channels.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A user wants the app homepage, news, live results, and notifications to reflect personal football interests.

### UN-004: I need current football news for my favorite teams and selected sources.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A fan checks the app during the day to stay informed about club news, match reports, and updates.

### UN-005: I need to follow live results and match events in real time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During a match, a fan uses the live ticker to track scores and key events as they happen.

### UN-006: I need extensive coverage of national and international leagues and competitions.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** A user follows multiple leagues and tournaments and expects a single app to cover them comprehensively.

### UN-007: I need detailed team and player information at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** A user researches team form, squad members, player statistics, and profile details before or after matches.

### UN-008: I need access to legally available live-stream links when a broadcast begins.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A fan wants to find an official provider link for a live broadcast directly from the match page.

### UN-009: I need notifications for important news and match events when enabled.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A user opts in to alerts for favorite-team goals, results, and breaking news while not actively using the app.

### UN-010: I need to share football news and game reports via social media.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Socially Active Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** A user reads an article or match report and wants to share it with friends directly from the app.

### UN-011: I need the app to remain usable when network coverage is limited or temporarily unavailable.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A user travels, enters a stadium, or experiences poor connectivity and still needs access to cached content and stable app navigation.

### UN-012: I need my personal data to be protected and processed lawfully.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** A user creates an account, sets preferences, enables notifications, and expects privacy, consent, and GDPR compliance.

### UN-013: I need a reliable app experience during major match-day traffic spikes and after release.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Many users access the app simultaneously during popular matches, and users expect stable service plus ongoing improvements from feedback.
