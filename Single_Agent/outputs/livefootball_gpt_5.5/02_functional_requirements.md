# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a native or cross-platform mobile application for Android and iOS devices.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register, log in, maintain an authenticated session, and log out from the app startup flow.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow authenticated users to select, update, and remove favorite football clubs.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and manage preferred sports channels used for personalized news and content filtering.
- **Source Need:** UN-003, UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to a user's favorite teams and selected sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a news detail view containing article title, source, publication time, body summary, related team tags, and share action.
- **Source Need:** UN-004, UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve live match data from an external server API through the live ticker module.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live scores, match clock, status, and chronological match events for selected favorite-team matches.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously update live ticker information while a match is active and network connectivity is available.
- **Source Need:** UN-005, UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall present national and international leagues and competitions with associated fixtures, results, and teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide searchable team profiles including overview, fixtures, recent results, squad list, and key statistics.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide player profiles including biography, team, position, nationality, and performance statistics where data is available.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live-stream provider links only after the live broadcast has begun.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall evaluate country-specific transmission-rights rules before displaying any live-stream provider link.
- **Source Need:** UN-008, UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-015
- **Requirement:** The system shall hide or disable unavailable live-stream links and explain that the stream is not available in the user's country or has not started.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-016
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to enable, disable, and configure notification categories for favorite-team news and results.
- **Source Need:** UN-009, UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-017
- **Requirement:** The system shall send notifications for configured current events when the notification function is activated and operating-system permission is granted.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-018
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and match reports through device-supported social media and messaging channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-019
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline access to previously loaded core content including favorites, recent news summaries, recent results, and team/player profiles.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-020
- **Requirement:** The system shall collect in-app feedback or direct users to app review channels so that feedback can be evaluated for continuous improvement.
- **Source Need:** UN-013
- **Priority:** Medium
