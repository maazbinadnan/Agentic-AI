# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Football fans need to log in quickly and easily at app startup so they can access all app features without friction
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** At app launch, users must complete registration or login before accessing news, live scores, team information, stream links, notifications, or personalization features.

### UN-002: Football fans need a fast-loading mobile app experience so they can start using the app within two seconds of opening it
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When opening the app on Android or iOS devices, users expect the app to become ready for immediate interaction with minimal delay.

### UN-003: Football fans need a central place to view current football news tailored to their favorite teams and preferred sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After logging in, users browse news content and want it filtered according to their selected clubs and chosen sports content sources.

### UN-004: Football fans need to follow live match results of their favorite teams in real time through a live ticker
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During live matches, users monitor ongoing results and match developments through continuously updated live ticker information.

### UN-005: Football fans need broad access to league, competition, team, and player information so they can stay comprehensively informed about football
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While exploring the app outside or alongside live matches, users look up national and international competitions as well as detailed team and player information.

### UN-006: Football fans need access to live stream links for active broadcasts when available in their country so they can continue viewing matches through approved providers
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a match is being broadcast live, users open the relevant match context in the app and use provided deep links to external streaming services where rights allow.

### UN-007: Football fans need to personalize the app by following favorite clubs and configuring content preferences so the app reflects their interests
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After account access, users set up favorite clubs and preferred channels to shape the content, updates, and overall experience shown in the app.

### UN-008: Football fans need to receive goal notifications for their favorite teams so they can stay updated on key match events without constantly monitoring the app
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After enabling notifications and selecting favorite teams, users receive alerts when goal events occur for those teams.

### UN-009: Football fans need to share news and game reports directly from the app to social media so they can engage others with football content
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** While reading news articles or match reports, users use built-in sharing options to distribute content through social platforms.

### UN-010: Football fans need the app to remain usable under poor connectivity and offline conditions by allowing access to cached news
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When network coverage is limited or temporarily unavailable, users continue accessing previously cached news content until connectivity returns.

### UN-011: Football fans need a consistent and responsive app experience even during peak match-day demand
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During periods of very high concurrent usage, users expect core app journeys such as login, browsing news, checking live scores, and viewing match information to continue without noticeable degradation.

### UN-012: Football fans need assurance that their personal data is handled in compliance with GDPR so they can trust the app with their account and preference information
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout registration, login, personalization, and notification use, users provide personal data and expect lawful, privacy-conscious handling of that information.
