# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall require users to register or log in at app startup before granting access to any app features.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall make the mobile application ready for user interaction within a maximum of two seconds after startup on supported Android and iOS devices under normal operating conditions.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow authenticated users to select favorite teams and preferred sports channels and shall display current football news filtered according to those selections.
- **Source Need:** UN-003, UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker that retrieves live match data from an external API and continuously updates live results for users' favorite teams in near real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall present coverage of available national and international leagues and competitions and shall provide detailed team and player information from the supported data source.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall display deep links to external live stream providers for active match broadcasts only when broadcast rights are valid for the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow authenticated users to manage personalization settings for favorite clubs and content preferences within the app interface.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to enable notifications and shall send notifications when goal events occur for the users' selected favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-008, UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports directly from the app through supported social media sharing mechanisms on the device.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to access previously cached news content when the device has limited or no network connectivity.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users on peak match days without loss of core application performance.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall process users' personal data in compliance with GDPR during registration, login, personalization, notification handling, and other account-related operations.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that presents and manages the app's modules together in a consistent structure that remains stable when external server data is updated.
- **Source Need:** UN-004, UN-005, UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium
