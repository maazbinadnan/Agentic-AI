# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: The app must load and be ready to use within two seconds of startup.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** App launch and initial access.

### UN-002: Users need to access comprehensive, up-to-date football news for their favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Browsing news and updates within the app.

### UN-003: Users must be able to select and follow their favorite teams and sports channels for personalized content.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Personalization and content selection during onboarding and ongoing use.

### UN-004: Users require live match results and updates via a real-time live ticker.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Following live matches and scores.

### UN-005: Users need access to live stream links for matches, with compliance to transmission rights.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Watching live matches and accessing streaming content.

### UN-006: Users must be able to retrieve live results in real time, even under limited network conditions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Using the app during poor network coverage or offline scenarios.

### UN-007: Users need detailed information about teams and players at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Researching teams and players, pre/post-match analysis.

### UN-008: Users require an intuitive user interface that connects and manages all app modules.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Navigating and interacting with app features.

### UN-009: Users must be able to configure the app and connect their own interface to the user interface.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Advanced Users / Customizers
- **Demand Level:** Low
- **User Journey Context:** Advanced customization and integration.

### UN-010: Users need to receive notifications about current events, news, and match results.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Staying informed via push notifications.

### UN-011: Registration and login must be uncomplicated and completed directly at app startup.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Onboarding and authentication.

### UN-012: Users must be able to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Sharing content with friends and followers.

### UN-013: The app must function reliably and remain usable offline, even with temporary loss of connection.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Using the app during network outages or in low coverage areas.

### UN-014: The app must support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance loss, especially on match days.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** High-traffic scenarios, such as major match days.

### UN-015: The app must undergo thorough testing and support processes to detect and resolve malfunctions early.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** System Administrators / Support Staff
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** App maintenance, quality assurance, and support.

### UN-016: User feedback and app reviews must be continuously evaluated to improve functionality.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Post-launch improvement and user engagement.

### UN-017: All personal data must be processed in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Registration, personalization, and ongoing data processing.
