# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds of app startup.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without degradation of performance, especially during high-traffic events such as major match days.
- **Source:** UN-014
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide live match updates via the live ticker with a maximum end-to-end latency of two seconds under normal network conditions.
- **Source:** UN-004, UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable and provide access to previously loaded content and core features when offline or during temporary loss of network connectivity.
- **Source:** UN-013, UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall process and store all personal user data in full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
- **Source:** UN-017
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall only display live stream links when transmission rights are fulfilled for the user’s country, ensuring compliance with all applicable content licensing agreements.
- **Source:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide an intuitive and consistent user interface that enables users to access all major features within three taps from the home screen.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow registration and login to be completed within 30 seconds, with no more than two user actions required after app startup.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo comprehensive functional and non-functional testing, with all critical defects resolved prior to release.
- **Source:** UN-015
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously collect and evaluate user feedback and app reviews to identify and implement improvements within a three-month release cycle.
- **Source:** UN-016
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to share news and game reports via social media platforms directly from the app within five seconds of initiating the share action.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow advanced users to configure and connect their own interface modules to the main user interface without impacting core app stability.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Low

### NFR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed team and player information with a maximum retrieval time of three seconds per request.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall deliver push notifications about current events, news, and match results to users within five seconds of the triggering event.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High
