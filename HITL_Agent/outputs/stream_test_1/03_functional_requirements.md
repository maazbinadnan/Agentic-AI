# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds of startup on both Android and iOS devices.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display comprehensive, up-to-date football news for users’ selected favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite teams and sports channels for personalized content delivery.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide real-time live match results and updates via a live ticker module.
- **Source Need:** UN-004, UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links for matches only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user’s country.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve and display live results in real time, even under limited network conditions.
- **Source Need:** UN-006, UN-013
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed information about teams and players at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide an intuitive user interface that connects and manages all app modules.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to configure the app and connect their own interface to the user interface.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Low

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall send notifications to users about current events, news, and match results when the notification function is activated.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide uncomplicated registration and login processes that can be completed directly at app startup.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably and remain usable offline, even with temporary loss of network connection.
- **Source Need:** UN-013
- **Priority:** High

### FR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance loss, especially during high-traffic events.
- **Source Need:** UN-014
- **Priority:** High

### FR-015
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes to detect and resolve malfunctions before release.
- **Source Need:** UN-015
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-016
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously evaluate user feedback and app reviews to improve functionality.
- **Source Need:** UN-016
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-017
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal data in compliance with the GDPR.
- **Source Need:** UN-017
- **Priority:** High
