# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds of startup on both Android and iOS devices.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a centralized platform displaying up-to-date football news, results, and events for all supported leagues and competitions.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and personalize their preferred sports channels and favorite teams during onboarding and via settings.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays real-time match results and updates for users’ favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to detailed team and player information at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for matches, displaying them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user’s country.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup using email/password and third-party authentication providers (e.g., Google, Apple, Facebook).
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to receive push notifications about current events, news, and results for their selected favorite teams, with explicit opt-in consent.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via major social media platforms using native OS share sheets.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably under limited network coverage and provide offline access to cached news and previously loaded data, while disabling live features when offline.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance degradation, especially during peak match days.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes prior to release to detect and resolve malfunctions early.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously evaluate user feedback and app reviews to identify and implement functionality improvements.
- **Source Need:** UN-013
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal user data in compliance with GDPR and applicable data protection standards, including privacy policy presentation and consent mechanisms.
- **Source Need:** UN-014
- **Priority:** High
