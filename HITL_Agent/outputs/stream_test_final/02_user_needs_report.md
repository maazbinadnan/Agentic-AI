# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: The app must load and be ready to use within two seconds of startup.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users expect immediate access to football information and features upon launching the app.

### UN-002: Users need a central platform to stay comprehensively informed about football news, results, and events.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users want to access up-to-date news, scores, and information about their favorite teams and competitions.

### UN-003: Users must be able to select and personalize preferred sports channels and favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users personalize their app experience to follow specific teams and channels, receiving tailored content.

### UN-004: Users need access to live ticker functionality for real-time match results and updates.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users follow live matches and receive instant updates on scores and events.

### UN-005: Users require detailed team and player information available at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users research teams and players for deeper engagement and knowledge.

### UN-006: Users need integrated live stream links for matches, with compliance to transmission rights.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users access live broadcasts directly from the app when rights are fulfilled in their country.

### UN-007: Users must be able to register and log in easily, directly at app startup.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users quickly create accounts or log in to personalize their experience and access features.

### UN-008: Users need to receive notifications about current events, news, and results for their favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users stay informed in real time via push notifications, enhancing engagement.

### UN-009: Users must be able to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Users share football content with friends and communities, increasing app reach.

### UN-010: The app must function reliably even with limited network coverage and remain usable offline.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users expect uninterrupted access to essential features, even in poor connectivity scenarios.

### UN-011: The app must support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance loss, especially on match days.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users experience consistent performance during peak usage periods.

### UN-012: The app must undergo thorough testing and support processes to detect and resolve malfunctions early.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** System Administrators
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Administrators ensure app reliability and user satisfaction by proactively managing issues.

### UN-013: User feedback and app reviews must be continuously evaluated to improve functionality.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** System Administrators
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Administrators monitor user input to guide app enhancements and address concerns.

### UN-014: All personal data must be processed in compliance with GDPR and data protection standards.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Users trust the app to safeguard their personal information and comply with legal requirements.
