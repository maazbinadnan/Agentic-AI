# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Football fans need to register and log in بسهولة at app startup before accessing app features
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When first opening the app, users must complete mandatory registration or login quickly and simply so they can begin using the service without friction.

### UN-002: Football fans need a personalized football information experience centered on their favorite teams and preferred sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After signing in, users configure favorites and channel preferences so the app surfaces the most relevant news and content for ongoing daily use.

### UN-003: Football fans need access to current football news about their favorite teams from selected sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During regular app use, users browse timely news content tailored to their interests in order to stay comprehensively informed.

### UN-004: Football fans need to follow live results and match events for their favorite teams in real time
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** On match days and during live fixtures, users monitor continuously updated scores and match developments through the live ticker.

### UN-005: Football fans need broad coverage across national and international leagues and competitions
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When exploring competitions beyond a single club or league, users expect the app to support a wide football-following journey across domestic and global events.

### UN-006: Football fans need detailed team and player information available at any time
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** While reading news, checking fixtures, or exploring clubs, users access team and player details to deepen their understanding of football topics.

### UN-007: Football fans need direct access to available live stream links for matches through approved external broadcast providers
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a live broadcast begins, users want to move from match tracking to viewing by opening eligible deep links to external streaming services.

### UN-008: Football fans need live stream links to be shown only when broadcasting rights are valid in their country
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When attempting to access match broadcasts, users need region-appropriate availability so presented stream options are lawful and accurate.

### UN-009: Football fans need to receive notifications about news and results for their favorite teams when they explicitly opt in
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After enabling notifications, users expect prompt alerts about relevant team events without receiving unwanted messages if they have not consented.

### UN-010: Football fans need to share news and match reports directly from the app via social media
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After consuming interesting content, users can distribute articles and reports to their social networks without leaving the app workflow.

### UN-011: Football fans need the app to load quickly and provide smooth access to information
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Each time the app is launched and navigated, users expect a responsive experience with the app ready to use within two seconds.

### UN-012: Football fans need the app to remain reliable under limited connectivity and still provide access to cached content and preferences offline
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When network coverage is weak or temporarily unavailable, users continue accessing previously loaded content and saved preferences until connectivity returns.

### UN-013: Football fans need the app to remain available and performant even during peak match-day demand
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During major football events and heavy concurrent usage, users still expect uninterrupted service and consistent responsiveness.

### UN-014: Football fans need their personal data and app usage to be handled in compliance with GDPR
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout registration, personalization, notifications, and ongoing app use, users need confidence that their personal data is protected and lawfully processed.

### UN-015: Product and support stakeholders need user feedback and app reviews to be continuously evaluated to improve the app
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Product and Support Stakeholders
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After release and during live operations, internal stakeholders review user feedback and ratings to identify issues and refine app functionality over time.
