# Visual & UI Design Requirements Specification

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Vision
- **Product Aesthetic Tone**: Clean Minimalist
- **Target Experience**: Mobile-First Football News & Live Ticker App (Android & iOS)

## 2. Color Theme & Palette
- **Mode Preference**: Dark Mode
- **Primary Color**: Dark Blue (#0F172A)
- **Secondary / Accent Colors**: Light Blue (#3B82F6), Green (#10B981)
- **Background & Surface**: Very Dark Blue (#1E293B)

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Primary Font Family**: Inter
- **Heading Hierarchy**:
  - H1: 2rem (32px), Bold
  - H2: 1.5rem (24px), Semi-bold
  - Body: 1rem (16px), Regular

## 4. Layout & UI Structure
- **Navigation Style**: Side Drawer (for main navigation)
- **Density**: Spacious (to support clean minimalist look and mobile readability)
- **Card & Container Style**: Rounded corners (12px), subtle shadow, minimalist borders

## 6. Documented Default Assumptions
- Accent colors (light blue, green) and background shades were selected to complement the primary dark blue and ensure accessibility in dark mode.
- Spacious layout and minimalist card/container style were chosen to align with the “clean minimalist” direction and mobile usability best practices.
