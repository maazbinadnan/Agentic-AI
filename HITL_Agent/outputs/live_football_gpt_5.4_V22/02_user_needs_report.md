# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Football fans need to access the app on both Android and iOS devices with a fast startup experience
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Mobile App User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When launching the app, users need the service to be available on their mobile platform and ready to use within two seconds so they can begin their session without delay.

### UN-002: Football fans need to complete mandatory registration and login بسهولة at app startup before using the app
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** At the beginning of the app journey, users must be able to register or log in quickly and directly from startup in order to access all app features.

### UN-003: Football fans need a central place to view current football news relevant to their favorite teams and preferred sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During regular content consumption, users want to stay informed by browsing up-to-date football news tailored to their selected interests.

### UN-004: Football fans need to follow live results of their favorite teams through a live ticker with real-time updates
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While matches are in progress, users want to monitor scores and match events continuously through an in-app live ticker.

### UN-005: Football fans need access to detailed team and player information across leagues and competitions
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When exploring football content beyond live updates, users want to look up background information about teams and players at any time.

### UN-006: Football fans need to open eligible live stream links to external broadcast providers when rights allow in their country
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a live match is being broadcast, users want to access available stream options from the app and be redirected only when rights and country availability permit it.

### UN-007: Football fans need to personalize the app by following favorite clubs, selecting preferred channels, and choosing relevant notifications
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After signing in and setting preferences, users want the app experience to reflect their interests so content and alerts are more relevant.

### UN-008: Football fans need to receive notifications about news and results for their favorite teams when notifications are activated
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Outside active app usage, users want timely alerts about important events related to their selected teams once they have enabled notifications.

### UN-009: Football fans need to share news and match reports directly from the app through social media
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Socially Active Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After reading news or reviewing match content, users want to distribute that content easily through their social platforms.

### UN-010: Football fans need the app to remain usable under limited connectivity and provide offline access to cached news, stories, and scores
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Mobile App User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When network coverage is weak or temporarily unavailable, users still want to access previously cached football content without losing basic usability.

### UN-011: Football fans need reliable app performance during peak match-day demand without noticeable service degradation
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Mobile App User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During high-traffic periods such as match days, users expect the app to continue serving core content and live features even when many people are using it simultaneously.

### UN-012: Registered users need their personal data and preferences to be handled in compliance with GDPR
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Registered User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout account creation, personalization, notifications, and ongoing app usage, users need confidence that their personal data is processed lawfully and protected appropriately.
