# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall require users to complete registration or login at app startup before granting access to any app features or content.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall load the mobile application and make it ready for user interaction within a maximum of two seconds after startup under normal supported device and network conditions.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news filtered according to each user's selected favorite teams and preferred sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-003, UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to personalize the app by selecting favorite clubs, choosing preferred sports channels, and configuring available app modules and interface preferences.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide an integrated live ticker that retrieves live match data from an external API and displays continuously updated real-time results and match events for users' selected teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide football content coverage across supported national and international leagues and competitions within the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall display detailed read-only team and player information on demand.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall display deep links to licensed external live stream providers for matches that are currently being broadcast and shall show such links only when the relevant transmission rights are valid in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to enable notifications and shall send alerts about relevant news updates and match results for their favorite teams based on their notification settings.
- **Source Need:** UN-009, UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news items and match reports directly from the app through native social media sharing mechanisms.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable under limited or absent network connectivity by providing offline access to previously cached news and team data only.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users during peak match-day demand without performance degradation in core app services.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall process, store, and manage users' personal data in compliance with GDPR requirements applicable to registration, authentication, personalization, notifications, and ongoing app usage.
- **Source Need:** UN-013, UN-001, UN-004, UN-009
- **Priority:** High
