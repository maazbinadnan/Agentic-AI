# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Access the app securely through a simple mandatory registration and login process before using any features
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** At app startup, users must quickly create an account or log in before entering the app and accessing personalized football content.

### UN-002: Receive a fast and smooth app experience that is ready to use within two seconds of launch
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When opening the app, users need the interface and core modules to load immediately so they can begin consuming content without delay.

### UN-003: Stay comprehensively informed through current football news centered on favorite teams and selected sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After logging in, users browse the app as a central football information hub and want relevant news tailored to their interests.

### UN-004: Personalize the app by following favorite clubs and configuring preferred content sources and modules
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During onboarding and ongoing use, users tailor the app interface and content setup so that the experience reflects their teams and preferred channels.

### UN-005: Follow live match results of favorite teams in real time through an integrated live ticker
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** On match days, users open the live ticker to monitor continuously updated scores and match events as they happen.

### UN-006: Access broad coverage across national and international leagues and competitions
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While exploring football content, users want the app to support the teams and competitions they care about across multiple regions and tournament types.

### UN-007: View detailed team and player information at any time
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When checking teams, matches, or news stories, users may drill down into team and player details for additional context.

### UN-008: Open live stream links for matches directly from the app when broadcasts are available from licensed providers in their country
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a match is live, users want a direct path from the app to an authorized streaming provider without needing to search elsewhere.

### UN-009: Receive timely notifications about relevant news and match results after enabling alerts
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After setting preferences and activating notifications, users expect prompt updates about events involving their favorite teams.

### UN-010: Share news and match reports directly from the app to social media
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Socially Active Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After reading news or reviewing match updates, users may want to distribute content quickly through their social platforms.

### UN-011: Continue using the app reliably under limited connectivity, including offline access to previously cached news and team data
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When network conditions are poor or temporarily unavailable, users still need the app to remain usable and provide cached information.

### UN-012: Experience stable access to app features and live content even during peak match-day demand
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During high-traffic events, users expect the app to maintain responsiveness and service quality without visible degradation.

### UN-013: Trust that their personal data is handled in compliance with GDPR and applicable privacy requirements
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Registered App User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout registration, personalization, and ongoing use, users need confidence that their personal data is processed lawfully and responsibly.
