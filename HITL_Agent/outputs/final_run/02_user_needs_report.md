# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Football fans need a fast-loading mobile app that is ready to use within two seconds of startup
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When launching the app on Android or iOS, users need immediate access to content without noticeable delay so they can quickly check football updates.

### UN-002: Football fans need a central platform to stay comprehensively informed about football news and updates
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During regular use of the app, users want one place to access football-related information without switching between multiple sources.

### UN-003: Football fans need to view current news about their favorite teams and preferred sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After entering the app and setting preferences, users want news feeds tailored to the teams and media sources they care about most.

### UN-004: Football fans need to follow live results of their favorite teams in real time through a live ticker
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While matches are in progress, users want continuously updated scores and match events so they can track games live from within the app.

### UN-005: Football fans need access to coverage across national and international leagues and competitions
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When browsing competitions and match content, users want broad coverage so they can follow teams and tournaments of interest regardless of geography.

### UN-006: Football fans need to access detailed team and player information at any time
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When exploring clubs, fixtures, or football news, users want supporting team and player details to deepen their understanding of the content.

### UN-007: Football fans need to access live stream links for matches directly in the app when broadcasts begin
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When a live match is available to watch, users want direct access to stream links inside the app so they can move quickly from following updates to viewing the broadcast.

### UN-008: Football fans need live stream links to be shown only when broadcasting rights are valid in their country
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When accessing streaming options, users need region-appropriate availability so the app presents only compliant and valid viewing links.

### UN-009: Football fans need a simple and direct registration and login process at app startup
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** At first use or when returning to the app, users want to sign up or log in quickly so they can begin using personalized features with minimal effort.

### UN-010: Football fans need to personalize their app experience by following favorite clubs
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During onboarding or settings configuration, users want to select favorite clubs so the app can prioritize relevant news, results, and updates.

### UN-011: Football fans need to receive notifications about news and results for their favorite teams when notifications are activated
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After enabling notifications, users want timely alerts about important developments involving their selected teams, even when they are not actively using the app.

### UN-012: Football fans need to share news and match reports directly from the app via social media
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Socially Active Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When users encounter interesting news or match content, they want to distribute it quickly through their social platforms without leaving the app workflow.

### UN-013: Football fans need the app to remain usable even with limited network coverage or temporary offline conditions
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When traveling, commuting, or experiencing weak connectivity, users want the app to continue functioning reliably so they can still access key features.

### UN-014: Football fans need a clear and directly accessible interface that brings together app modules consistently
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout app usage, users want an intuitive interface that presents news, live ticker, notifications, and personalization features in a coherent and manageable way.

### UN-015: Football fans need the app to remain responsive and available during peak match-day usage
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During high-interest events with many concurrent users, users want uninterrupted access to app content and live updates without performance degradation.

### UN-016: Football fans need assurance that their personal data is handled in compliance with GDPR
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Registered User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When registering, logging in, personalizing preferences, or receiving notifications, users want confidence that their personal data is collected and processed lawfully and securely.
