# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within a maximum of 2 seconds after application startup on supported Android and iOS devices under normal operating conditions.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall update and display live ticker match data in real time with end-to-end data refresh latency low enough to present current match events continuously during active matches.
- **Source:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable during periods of limited network coverage and shall preserve access to core app functionality and previously retrieved content when connectivity is temporarily unavailable.
- **Source:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall support at least 100,000 concurrent users on peak match days without performance loss affecting core user functions, including news access, live ticker updates, and team information retrieval.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links only when transmission rights are valid for the user’s country and shall prevent presentation of restricted streams where rights are not fulfilled.
- **Source:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal data in compliance with GDPR requirements, including lawful processing, data minimization, and protection of user privacy for registration, personalization, notifications, and account-related data.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that enables users to access core information and functions directly and consistently across integrated modules on supported Android and iOS devices.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain functional consistency of the user interface and internal modules despite updates received from external servers.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall support uncomplicated registration and login at application startup with a streamlined authentication flow requiring minimal user steps.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall deliver notifications about current events to users as soon as the notification function is activated and relevant subscribed events occur.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough pre-release testing and support processes to detect and resolve malfunctions before production release.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall support continuous evaluation of user feedback and app reviews to inform ongoing improvements to app functionality and service quality.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Low
