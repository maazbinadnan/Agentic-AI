# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide the mobile application on Android and iOS platforms and make the application ready for user interaction within a maximum of two seconds after startup under normal supported operating conditions.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall require users to register and log in at app startup before accessing application functionality.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a unified user interface that presents and manages the app’s core modules, including news, live ticker, team and player information, stream links, notifications, and personalization features, through a consistent and directly accessible interface.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-014
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow authenticated users to select favorite clubs and preferred supported sports channels to personalize the content presented in the application.
- **Source Need:** UN-003, UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news filtered according to the authenticated user’s selected favorite clubs and preferred supported sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide coverage data for supported national and international football leagues and competitions.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed information pages for teams and players and make them accessible at any time from within the application.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve live match data from an external API and display live results and match events for users’ favorite teams through an integrated live ticker module.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously update the live ticker display with current match data as new live data is received from the external API.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall display eligible external live stream links for matches directly in the application when the corresponding live broadcast has begun.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links only when broadcast rights metadata confirms that the stream is permitted in the user’s country.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to manually activate notifications for their selected favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall send notifications about news and live results only for the user’s selected favorite teams when the user has activated notifications.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share supported news items and match reports through the mobile device’s social sharing capabilities directly from the application.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-015
- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable during limited network coverage or temporary loss of connectivity by allowing access to available application interface components and previously loaded content.
- **Source Need:** UN-013
- **Priority:** High

### FR-016
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users during peak match-day usage without loss of core application performance.
- **Source Need:** UN-015
- **Priority:** High

### FR-017
- **Requirement:** The system shall process users’ personal data, including account data, preferences, and notification settings, in compliance with GDPR.
- **Source Need:** UN-016
- **Priority:** High

### FR-018
- **Requirement:** The system shall keep the user interface and application modules operational and structurally consistent when external server data is updated.
- **Source Need:** UN-014
- **Priority:** Medium
