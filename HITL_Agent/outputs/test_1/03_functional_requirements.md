# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within a maximum of two seconds after startup.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a central platform displaying comprehensive football news at all times.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to display current football news of their favorite teams and individually select preferred sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to follow live results of their favorite teams via a real-time live ticker.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide extensive coverage of all leagues and competitions, both national and international.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to detailed team and player information at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links from providers (e.g., DAZN, Sky Sport) and display them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve and display live results of users' favorite teams in real time, even with limited network coverage.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that allows direct access to all information and modules, supporting seamless navigation.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to personalize their experience by following favorite clubs and receiving notifications about news or results.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably with limited network coverage and remain usable offline if the connection is temporarily lost.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance loss, especially on match days.
- **Source Need:** UN-013
- **Priority:** High

### FR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide uncomplicated registration and login processes that can be completed directly at app startup.
- **Source Need:** UN-014
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-015
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes before release to detect and resolve malfunctions early.
- **Source Need:** UN-015
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-016
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously evaluate user feedback and app reviews to improve functionality.
- **Source Need:** UN-016
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-017
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal data in compliance with the GDPR.
- **Source Need:** UN-017
- **Priority:** High
