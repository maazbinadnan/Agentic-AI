# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: The app must load and be ready to use within a maximum of two seconds after startup.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Initial app launch and onboarding; ensures immediate access and positive first impression.

### UN-002: Users need a central platform to stay comprehensively informed about football news at all times.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Daily information retrieval; browsing news and updates about favorite teams and leagues.

### UN-003: Users must be able to display current football news of their favorite teams and select preferred sports channels individually.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Customizing news feed and channel preferences for personalized content consumption.

### UN-004: Users need to follow live results of their favorite teams via a live ticker in real time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Real-time match tracking during games; instant updates on scores and events.

### UN-005: The app must offer extensive coverage of all leagues and competitions, both national and international.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Accessing information across diverse football competitions; ensuring inclusivity and breadth.

### UN-006: Users need access to detailed team and player information at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Researching teams and players; pre-match analysis and general curiosity.

### UN-007: The app must integrate live stream links from providers (e.g., DAZN, Sky Sport) and display them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Content Providers
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Ensuring legal compliance and proper access to live broadcasts; stream integration workflow.

### UN-008: Users need the option to retrieve live results of their favorite teams in real time, even with limited network coverage.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Match day experience; maintaining access to live data under varying network conditions.

### UN-009: The user interface must allow direct access to all information and modules, supporting seamless navigation.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Navigating the app; accessing news, live ticker, streams, and personalization features.

### UN-010: Users must be able to personalize their experience by following favorite clubs and receiving notifications about news or results.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Setting preferences; receiving timely updates and alerts.

### UN-011: Users need to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Social engagement; sharing content with friends and communities.

### UN-012: The app must function reliably even with limited network coverage and remain usable offline if connection is temporarily lost.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Using the app in areas with poor connectivity; ensuring continuous access to core features.

### UN-013: The app must be able to serve up to 100,000 users simultaneously without performance loss, especially on match days.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** High-traffic events; maintaining stability and responsiveness during peak usage.

### UN-014: Registration and login must be uncomplicated and completed directly at app startup.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Onboarding; quick and easy access to personalized features.

### UN-015: The app must undergo thorough testing and support processes before release to detect and resolve malfunctions early.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** System Administrators / App Support Staff
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Pre-release quality assurance; ongoing maintenance and troubleshooting.

### UN-016: User feedback and app reviews must be continuously evaluated to improve functionality.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** System Administrators / App Support Staff
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Post-release improvement; monitoring and responding to user input.

### UN-017: All personal data must be processed in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Data privacy and security; ensuring legal compliance and user trust.
