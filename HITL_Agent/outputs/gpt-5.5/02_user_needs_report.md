# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Users need uncomplicated mandatory registration and login before accessing LiveFootball features
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** At app startup, users must authenticate through a simple registration/login flow before using news, live ticker, team/player information, personalization, notifications, sharing, or stream-link features, reflecting the clarified scope that no core feature is available anonymously.

### UN-002: Users need the app to start quickly and provide smooth access on Android and iOS
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Mobile App Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When launching the app on Android or iOS under normal conditions, users need the app to be fully loaded and ready to use within a maximum of two seconds so they can immediately access football information without perceived delay.

### UN-003: Users need a personalized football news experience based on favorite teams and preferred sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After login, users need to follow favorite clubs and select preferred sports channels so the app can display relevant football news in a central, personalized feed aligned with their interests.

### UN-004: Users need to follow live results and match events for favorite teams in near real time
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Match-Day Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During live matches, users need a live ticker that retrieves external match data and continuously updates scores and key match events for their favorite teams, within the MVP scope of top European leagues and competitions.

### UN-005: Users need access to team, player, league, and competition information for covered European football competitions
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While browsing the app, users need detailed team and player information and coverage of relevant leagues and competitions, with initial release coverage clarified as top European leagues and competitions rather than all global competitions.

### UN-006: Users need rights-compliant live broadcast access through authorized third-party deep links
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a live broadcast has begun, users need the app to show available links to authorized providers such as DAZN or Sky Sport only where transmission rights are fulfilled in the user’s country, with the clarified scope limited to deep links rather than hosted or embedded streams.

### UN-007: Users need opt-in notifications about current events, news, and results for favorite teams
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After choosing favorite clubs and activating notifications, users need timely alerts about current events such as news, live results, or match updates related to their favorite teams, subject to device permissions and network availability.

### UN-008: Users need to share football news and match reports directly through social media
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Social Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** While reading news or match reports in the app, users need a direct sharing option that lets them distribute content through social media or native mobile sharing mechanisms without leaving the LiveFootball journey unnecessarily.

### UN-009: Users need continued read-only access to previously loaded content during limited or lost connectivity
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Mobile Football Fan in Low-Connectivity Conditions
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When network coverage is limited or temporarily unavailable, users need the app to remain usable by displaying previously loaded or cached content only, with no requirement to perform new server-dependent actions while offline.

### UN-010: Users need reliable service during high-traffic match days
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Football Fans
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** On match days with heavy demand, users need the app experience to remain responsive and stable while serving up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance loss, especially for live scores, news access, and notifications.

### UN-011: Users need confidence that personal data is processed lawfully and protected under GDPR
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Registered User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout registration, personalization, notification use, and feedback-related interactions, users need their personal data to be processed in compliance with GDPR, including appropriate protection and lawful handling of account and preference data.

### UN-012: Users need the app to improve through evaluated feedback and app reviews
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Product Stakeholder
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** After release and during ongoing operation, product stakeholders need user feedback and app reviews to be continuously evaluated so defects, usability issues, and improvement opportunities can inform future functionality enhancements.
