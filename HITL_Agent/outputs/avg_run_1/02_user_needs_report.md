# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: Access the football app quickly after launch with a smooth startup experience
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When opening the mobile app on Android or iOS, users need the app to be ready to use within a very short time so they can immediately begin browsing football content.

### UN-002: Log in and register easily before using the app
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** At app startup, users need an uncomplicated authentication process so they can gain access to the app and proceed into the main experience without friction.

### UN-003: Stay comprehensively informed about football from a single central platform
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** During regular app usage, users want one place to access football-related content instead of relying on multiple separate sources.

### UN-004: View current news about favorite teams and preferred sports channels
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After entering the app and setting preferences, users want relevant football news tailored to the teams and channels they follow.

### UN-005: Follow live results of favorite teams in real time through a live ticker
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** While matches are in progress, users need continuously updated live score information so they can track match developments as they happen.

### UN-006: Access broad coverage of leagues, competitions, teams, and players
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** When exploring football content, users want coverage across national and international competitions and the ability to look up detailed team and player information at any time.

### UN-007: Open authorized live stream links for available matches from within the app
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When a live broadcast has begun, users want to find and open eligible stream links directly from the app and be redirected to the licensed provider where rights are valid in their country.

### UN-008: Personalize the app by following favorite clubs and configuring content preferences
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Early in the user journey and during continued use, users need to tailor the app to their interests so the interface and content reflect the teams and channels they care about most.

### UN-009: Receive notifications about live score updates and news for favorite teams
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** After enabling notifications, users want timely alerts about important events related to their selected teams even when they are not actively using the app.

### UN-010: Share news and match reports externally through social media
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** When users discover content worth discussing, they want to quickly share news items and game reports from the app to their social platforms.

### UN-011: Continue using the app reliably under poor connectivity or temporary offline conditions
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** In low-network situations or temporary disconnections, users need the app to remain usable by allowing access to previously cached content and maintaining a dependable experience.

### UN-012: Trust that personal data is handled securely and in compliance with privacy regulations
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Throughout registration, personalization, and ongoing app usage, users need confidence that their personal information is processed in accordance with GDPR requirements.
