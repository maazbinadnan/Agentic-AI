## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 2. Functional Requirements

---

**FR-001: Mobile App Startup and Access**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a mobile application for Android and iOS devices and present the initial access interface at app startup.
- **Source:** UN-001, UN-006
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-002: User Registration and Login**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly from app startup.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-003: Favorite Teams and Preferred Channels Management**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select, save, and manage favorite teams and preferred sports channels for personalized content delivery.
- **Source:** UN-002, UN-007
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-004: Football News Delivery**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news related to users’ favorite teams and selected sports channels.
- **Source:** UN-002
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-005: Live Ticker and Real-Time Match Updates**

- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve live match data from an external API and display continuously updated live ticker information for users’ selected or favorite teams.
- **Source:** UN-003, UN-011
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-006: League, Competition, Team, and Player Information**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to national and international leagues and competitions and display detailed information about teams and players.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-007: Live Stream Link Availability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links from supported providers when a live broadcast has begun and the transmission rights are fulfilled in the user’s country.
- **Source:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-008: Modular User Interface Configuration**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that integrates fixed app modules and allow users to configure available modules for a personalized app experience.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-009: Notification Management and Delivery**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to activate notifications and shall send notifications about relevant news, results, or current events related to their favorite teams.
- **Source:** UN-007, UN-011
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-010: Social Media Sharing**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports directly from the app via supported social media channels.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Could Have

---

**FR-011: Offline Usability Support**

- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable during temporary loss of network connection and shall continue to provide access to available in-app content and interface components when offline.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-012: Feedback and Support Intake**

- **Requirement:** The system shall support the collection of user feedback and app review information for ongoing functional improvement and support analysis.
- **Source:** UN-013
- **Priority:** Should Have
