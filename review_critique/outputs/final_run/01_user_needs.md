## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 1. Discovered User Needs

**UN-001**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** At app startup and during regular daily use, football fans need fast access to a central platform that consolidates football information so they can immediately begin using the app without delay.

**UN-002**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** While following football activity, users want to view current news about their favorite teams and tailor content sources by selecting preferred sports channels.

**UN-003**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** During live matches, users want to follow the real-time results and match events of their favorite teams through a live ticker that updates continuously.

**UN-004**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** When exploring football content, users want broad coverage across national and international leagues and competitions, including detailed information about teams and players.

**UN-005**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** When a live match is being broadcast, users want to access relevant live stream links from supported providers directly in the app, where legally permitted in their country.

**UN-006**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** At first launch and subsequent app access, users want a simple registration and login process so that they can quickly enter the app and personalize their experience.

**UN-007**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** After signing in, users want to personalize the app by following favorite clubs, configuring modules, and enabling notifications relevant to their interests.

**UN-008**
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** After reading football news or match reports, users want to share content through social media directly from the app.

**UN-009**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need the app to remain reliable and usable under limited connectivity or temporary loss of network access.

**UN-010**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** During periods of peak demand such as match days, users need the app to continue performing without degradation while many users access it simultaneously.

**UN-011**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need timely awareness of important match events, results, or news updates through notifications when they choose to enable them.

**UN-012**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Throughout registration, personalization, and ongoing usage, users need their personal data to be handled securely and in compliance with GDPR.

**UN-013**
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Product and Support Team
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Before and after release, the business needs testing, support, and feedback evaluation processes to identify issues early and improve app functionality over time.
