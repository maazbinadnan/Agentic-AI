## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 3. Non-Functional Requirements

---

**NFR-001: Startup Performance**

- **Requirement:** The system shall be fully loaded and ready for user interaction within 2 seconds of app launch under normal operating conditions.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-002: Real-Time Update Responsiveness**

- **Requirement:** The system shall process and display newly retrieved live ticker data without requiring manual refresh by the user while an active network connection is available.
- **Source:** UN-003, UN-011
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-003: Reliability Under Limited Connectivity**

- **Requirement:** The system shall remain operational during temporary network interruptions and shall not crash when connectivity is lost or restored.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-004: Scalability at Peak Load**

- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users on peak match days without performance loss in core user journeys.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-005: Platform Compatibility**

- **Requirement:** The system shall operate on both Android and iOS mobile platforms.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-006: UI Consistency and Module Stability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain a consistent user interface and fixed core modules even when external server data is updated.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-007: Legal and Rights Compliance for Streams**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links only when transmission rights are fulfilled for the user’s country.
- **Source:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-008: Data Protection and Privacy Compliance**

- **Requirement:** The system shall process personal data in compliance with GDPR.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-009: Quality Assurance and Support Readiness**

- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo testing and support readiness activities before release to identify and resolve malfunctions early.
- **Source:** UN-013
- **Priority:** Medium
