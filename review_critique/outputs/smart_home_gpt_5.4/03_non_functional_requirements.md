## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 3. Non-Functional Requirements

---

**NFR-001: Simulated Test Environment**

- **Requirement:** The system shall be developed and tested in a simulated environment with no actual physical home, and all sensors and controllers shall be simulated while adhering to the physical properties and constraints of an actual home and real sensors and controllers.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-002: Prototype Delivery Constraint**

- **Requirement:** The prototype version of the DigitalHome system shall be completed within twelve months of project inception.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

---

**NFR-003: Development Team Constraint**

- **Requirement:** The DigitalHome prototype project shall be executed by a development team consisting of five engineers.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-004: Project Management Support Constraint**

- **Requirement:** The DigitalHomeOwner Director shall provide management and communication support to the development team during the project.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-005: Mandated Development Process**

- **Requirement:** The development team shall use the development process specified by Digital HomeOwner Inc.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-006: Technology and Standards Constraint**

- **Requirement:** The system shall employ widely used, accepted, and available hardware and software technologies and standards where possible for product elements and development tools.
- **Source:** UN-004, UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-007: Cost Minimization Constraint**

- **Requirement:** The project shall minimize the cost of DigitalHome elements, including sensors, controllers, servers, and tools, and the final project report shall document cost-minimization efforts and price comparisons with comparable elements.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-008: Network Dependency**

- **Requirement:** The operational environment shall require a widely available broadband Internet Service Provider connection such as cable modem or high-speed DSL.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-009: Gateway Wireless Range**

- **Requirement:** The gateway device shall support indoor wireless transmission over a range of up to 1000 feet.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-010: Thermostat Operating Range**

- **Requirement:** The thermostat sensor component shall support a sensitivity range from 14°F to 104°F (-10°C to 40°C).
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

---

**NFR-011: Change Control Governance**

- **Requirement:** Major changes to the requirements specification shall require approval from the Director of the DigitalHomeOwner Division.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium
