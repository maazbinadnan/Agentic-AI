## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 2. Functional Requirements

---

**FR-001: Mobile App Availability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide the LiveFootball application for Android and iOS mobile devices.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-002: Personalized News Feed**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to view current football news filtered by their favorite teams and selected sports channels.
- **Source:** UN-002
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-003: Favorite Team and Channel Configuration**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select, update, and save favorite clubs and preferred sports channels for personalized content delivery.
- **Source:** UN-002
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-004: Live Ticker Display**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves live match data from an external API and displays continuously updated match results and events for favorite teams.
- **Source:** UN-003
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-005: League, Competition, Team, and Player Information**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to national and international league and competition coverage, including detailed team and player information.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-006: Live Stream Link Availability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links within the app when a live broadcast has begun and an eligible provider link is available.
- **Source:** UN-005, UN-006
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-007: Transmission Rights Validation**

- **Requirement:** The system shall restrict the display of live stream links to users in countries where the respective transmission rights are fulfilled.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-008: Notification Delivery**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to activate notifications and receive updates about current events, news, and results related to their favorite teams.
- **Source:** UN-007, UN-002
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-009: Registration and Login**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at application startup.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-010: Social Media Sharing**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports to supported social media platforms directly from the app.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Could Have

---

**FR-011: Offline Usability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable when network connectivity is limited or temporarily unavailable.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-012: Modular User Interface Integration**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that integrates the live ticker, personalization, notification, and other app modules into a consistent access point for users.
- **Source:** UN-001, UN-003, UN-007
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-013: Feedback Intake Support**

- **Requirement:** The system shall support the collection of user feedback and app review inputs for product improvement.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-014: Personal Data Processing Controls**

- **Requirement:** The system shall process personal data used for registration, login, personalization, and notifications in accordance with applicable GDPR obligations.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Must Have
