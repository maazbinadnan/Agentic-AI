## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 1. Discovered User Needs

**UN-001**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** At app startup and during ongoing use, users need quick access to a central football information platform on their mobile device.

**UN-002**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users want to personalize the app by selecting favorite teams and preferred sports channels so that the information shown is relevant to them.

**UN-003**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** While following matches, users need to view live results and continuously updated match events for their favorite teams in real time.

**UN-004**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users want broad football coverage across national and international leagues and competitions, including detailed team and player information.

**UN-005**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** When broadcasts are available, users want to access live stream links for matches directly in the app.

**UN-006**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business / Compliance Stakeholders
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** The business needs live stream availability to comply with country-specific transmission rights before links are shown to users.

**UN-007**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Users want to receive notifications about current events, news, or match results when they enable notifications.

**UN-008**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Users want uncomplicated registration and login directly at app startup so they can begin using the app quickly.

**UN-009**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan / App User
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Users want to share news and game reports directly from the app to social media.

**UN-010**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business / Operations Stakeholders
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** The app must remain reliable and usable under limited network coverage, temporary offline conditions, and peak traffic on match days.

**UN-011**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Privacy / Compliance Stakeholders
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** User personal data must be handled in compliance with GDPR requirements.

**UN-012**
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Product / Support Team
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** After release preparation and during operation, the team needs testing, support, and feedback evaluation to identify issues and improve the app.
