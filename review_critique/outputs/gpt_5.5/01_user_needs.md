## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 1. Discovered User Needs

**UN-001**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** At app startup and during regular use, users need fast access to football content so they can begin using the app almost immediately on mobile devices.

**UN-002**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users want a central place to stay informed about football by viewing current news related to their favorite teams and selected sports channels.

**UN-003**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** While matches are in progress, users want to follow live results and continuously updated match events for their favorite teams in real time.

**UN-004**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Users want broad football coverage across national and international leagues and competitions so they can track a wide range of teams and tournaments in one app.

**UN-005**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Users want to access detailed information about teams and players whenever needed to better understand clubs, squads, and match context.

**UN-006**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** When a live broadcast is available, users want to discover and access live stream links from approved providers directly within the app.

**UN-007**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered User
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** At first launch or app startup, users want uncomplicated registration and login so they can quickly access personalized features.

**UN-008**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered User
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** After signing in, users want to personalize the app by following favorite clubs, selecting preferred sports channels, configuring modules, and enabling notifications.

**UN-009**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered User
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Users want to receive notifications about relevant news and match events when they have activated notifications.

**UN-010**
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Social Media Sharing User
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** While reading news or game reports, users want to share content directly via social media.

**UN-011**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need the app to remain reliable and usable under limited network coverage or temporary loss of connection.

**UN-012**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** On peak match days, users need the service to remain responsive even when many users access it at the same time.

**UN-013**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need their personal data to be processed lawfully and securely in compliance with GDPR.

**UN-014**
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Product and Support Team
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** Before and after release, the organization needs testing, support, and feedback evaluation processes to detect issues early and improve functionality over time.
