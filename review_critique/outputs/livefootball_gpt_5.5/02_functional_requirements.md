## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 2. Functional Requirements

---

**FR-001: Mobile App Platform Availability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide the LiveFootball application for Android and iOS mobile devices.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-002: Football News Feed Delivery**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news related to a user's favorite teams and individually selected sports channels.
- **Source:** UN-002, UN-008
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-003: Live Ticker and Real-Time Results**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide an integrated live ticker module that retrieves live match data from an external API and displays continuously updated live results for users' favorite teams.
- **Source:** UN-003
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-004: League and Competition Coverage**

- **Requirement:** The system shall present football content across national and international leagues and competitions.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-005: Team and Player Information Access**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to retrieve detailed team and player information at any time.
- **Source:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-006: Rights-Aware Live Stream Link Display**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links from approved broadcast providers only after the live broadcast has begun and only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-007: Registration and Login**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-008: Personalization and Module Configuration**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to personalize the app by following favorite clubs, selecting preferred sports channels, configuring available modules, and activating notification preferences.
- **Source:** UN-008, UN-009
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-009: Notification Delivery**

- **Requirement:** The system shall send notifications about current news and match results to users who have activated the notification function.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-010: Social Media Sharing**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports directly from the app via social media channels supported by the device.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-011: Offline Usability Support**

- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain core app usability during temporary loss of network connectivity.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-012: In-App Feedback Submission and Review**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to submit in-app feedback and shall make submitted feedback available for review by the product/support team for ongoing functional improvement.
- **Source:** UN-014
- **Priority:** Could Have
