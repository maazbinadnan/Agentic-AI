## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 2. Functional Requirements

---

**FR-001: User Registration and Login**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide users with the ability to register and log in from app startup to access personalized app features.
- **Source:** UN-002, UN-011
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-002: Favorite Teams and Channel Preferences**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and manage favorite clubs and preferred sports channels for personalized content delivery.
- **Source:** UN-003
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-003: Personalized Football News Display**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user’s selected favorite teams and preferred sports channels.
- **Source:** UN-001, UN-003
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-004: Live Ticker for Match Results and Events**

- **Requirement:** The system shall retrieve live match data from an external API and display continuously updated live results and match events for users’ selected teams through an integrated live ticker module.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-005: League, Competition, Team, and Player Information**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide users with access to national and international league and competition coverage, including detailed team and player information.
- **Source:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-006: Rights-Aware Live Stream Link Display**

- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links within the app when a live broadcast has begun and when transmission rights permit display in the user’s country.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-007: Event Notifications**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to activate notifications and receive alerts about current events, news, or results related to their favorite teams.
- **Source:** UN-007, UN-003
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-008: Social Media Sharing**

- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news and game reports directly from the app via social media channels supported by the device.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** Should Have

---

**FR-009: Unified and Stable Mobile User Interface**

- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a mobile user interface that presents and manages the app’s information modules in an integrated and directly accessible manner, and shall preserve the consistency of fixed user interface components and integrated modules when external server data or server-side updates change.
- **Source:** UN-001, UN-009
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-010: Offline Usability**

- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable when network connectivity is temporarily unavailable by allowing access to app functionality that does not require an active server response.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Must Have

---

**FR-011: Feedback and Review Evaluation Support**

- **Requirement:** The system shall support the ongoing evaluation of user feedback and app reviews for continuous improvement of app functionality.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** Could Have
