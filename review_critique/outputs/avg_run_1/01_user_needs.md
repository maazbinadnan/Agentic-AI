## Business Analysis & Requirements Specification

### 1. Discovered User Needs

**UN-001**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football fans
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** At app startup and during routine use, users need a central mobile platform to quickly access football information relevant to their interests.

**UN-002**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Registered app users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** After launching the app, users need a simple registration and login process so they can access personalized features without friction.

**UN-003**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Personalized content users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users want to configure favorite teams and preferred sports channels so the app presents relevant football news and updates.

**UN-004**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Live match followers
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** During matches, users need to follow live results and match events for favorite teams in real time through a live ticker.

**UN-005**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football information seekers
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** While browsing the app, users need broad coverage of national and international leagues and competitions, including detailed team and player information.

**UN-006**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Live content viewers
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** When live broadcasts begin, users need access to relevant live stream links in the app where transmission rights permit display in their country.

**UN-007**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Notification subscribers
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** After enabling notifications, users need timely alerts about current events, news, or results related to their favorite teams.

**UN-008**
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Socially active users
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** After reading news or game reports, users want to share content directly from the app via social media.

**UN-009**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All app users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need the app to load quickly, remain responsive, and continue functioning reliably under weak or absent network connectivity.

**UN-010**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All app users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need the service to remain available and performant during peak usage, such as match days with very high concurrent traffic.

**UN-011**
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All app users
- **demand:** High
- **User Journey:** Users need their personal data to be processed securely and in compliance with GDPR while using registration, personalization, and notification features.

**UN-012**
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** Product and support stakeholders
- **demand:** Medium
- **User Journey:** After release and during operations, stakeholders need defects to be identified early and user feedback to be evaluated continuously for app improvement.
