# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access football news, live scores, and team/player information quickly and smoothly so that I can stay informed about my favorite teams and competitions.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app to check news, scores, and information about their favorite teams and competitions.

### UN-002: I want to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams, sports channels, notification preferences, and news types so that I receive relevant news and live updates.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User configures preferences for teams, channels, notification types, and news categories after registration or in settings.

### UN-003: I want to receive real-time notifications about news and results for my favorite teams so that I never miss important updates.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications and receives push alerts for selected teams and events.

### UN-004: I want to watch live streams of matches when available and permitted in my country so that I can follow games directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses live stream links for matches, with access controlled by transmission rights and location.

### UN-005: I want to share news and match reports via social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp, Instagram) so that I can engage with my friends and community.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads news or reports and shares them directly from the app to supported social platforms.

### UN-006: I want the app to function reliably and remain usable even with limited or intermittent network coverage so that I can access information anytime, anywhere.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses the app in areas with poor connectivity and expects core features to remain available.

### UN-007: I need the app to support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance loss so that we can handle high traffic on match days.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business Owner/Product Manager
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Large numbers of users access the app simultaneously during peak events.

### UN-008: I need all user data to be processed in compliance with GDPR so that we protect user privacy and meet legal requirements.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business Owner/Product Manager
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers, logs in, and uses the app, with all data handled according to GDPR.

### UN-009: I want to register and log in quickly and easily, including with third-party authentication (Google, Apple, Facebook), so that I can start using the app without unnecessary delays.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads the app and completes registration/login at startup using email/password or third-party authentication.

### UN-010: I want the app to be thoroughly tested and improved based on user feedback so that malfunctions are minimized and user satisfaction increases.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business Owner/Product Manager
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** App undergoes QA testing before release and is updated based on ongoing user reviews and feedback.

### UN-011: I want the app to support localization for English, German, Spanish, French, and Italian so that it is accessible to users in our target markets.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business Owner/Product Manager
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User selects or is auto-detected for their preferred language during onboarding or in settings.

### UN-012: I want to access previously loaded news, scores, and team/player information offline for at least 7 days so that I can stay informed even without internet.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan/User
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app offline and expects to see cached news, scores, and team/player info from the past 7 days.

### UN-013: I want live stream links to open in an in-app browser or native player for a seamless viewing experience.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** Business Owner/Product Manager
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User taps a live stream link and the stream opens within the app, not in an external browser.
