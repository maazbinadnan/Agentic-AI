# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall achieve a cold start time (from app launch to main dashboard ready) of no more than 2 seconds on supported devices (Android/iOS).
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain 99.9% uptime during peak match days.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall ensure all user data is encrypted in transit and at rest, in accordance with GDPR.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that is consistent and accessible across all modules and device types.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall remain responsive and usable with network latency up to 500ms and during temporary loss of connectivity.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall support localization for all major languages relevant to target markets.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** Medium

### NFR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall log all access to live stream links for audit and compliance purposes.
- **Source:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium
