# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and display the main dashboard with news, live scores, and team/player information within 2 seconds of app launch.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and manage their favorite teams and sports channels for personalized content.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide real-time notifications to users about news and results for their selected favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links for matches only when the broadcast is available and permitted in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to share news articles and match reports directly to social media platforms from within the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline access to previously loaded news, scores, and team/player information when network connectivity is unavailable.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without degradation in performance during peak usage.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal user data in compliance with GDPR requirements.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup with a streamlined process.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo comprehensive testing before release and support continuous improvement based on user feedback and app reviews.
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium
