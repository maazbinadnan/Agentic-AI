# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite football teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays real-time match results for the user's favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed team and player information accessible at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for matches, displaying them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to enable or disable notifications for news and results of their favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline access to previously loaded news, results, and team/player information.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High
