# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access current football news and live results for my favorite teams so that I can stay informed at all times.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app, selects favorite teams, and views news and live results on the dashboard.

### UN-002: I want to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User configures preferences during onboarding or in settings to tailor content.

### UN-003: I want to watch live streams of football matches when available and permitted in my country.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User navigates to a match and accesses live stream links if rights are available.

### UN-004: I want to receive real-time notifications about news and results for my favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications and receives push alerts for relevant events.

### UN-005: I want to share news and game reports with friends via social media.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads an article and uses the share function to post on social media.

### UN-006: I want the app to load quickly and run smoothly, even during high traffic or poor network conditions.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User launches the app and expects it to be ready within 2 seconds, regardless of network or load.

### UN-007: I want to access team and player information at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User searches for or selects a team/player to view detailed information.

### UN-008: I want to use the app offline if I temporarily lose my internet connection.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User continues to browse cached content when offline.

### UN-009: I want my personal data to be protected and processed in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers, logs in, and uses the app, expecting privacy and data protection.

### UN-010: I want the app to be reliable and free from technical malfunctions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User interacts with the app and expects consistent, error-free operation.

### UN-011: I need to ensure that the app can support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance loss.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** System Administrator
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** Administrator monitors app performance during peak match days.
