# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite football teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays real-time match results for the user's favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed team and player information accessible at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for matches, displaying them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country using a geo-IP lookup or rights API.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup using email/password and third-party logins (Google, Apple).
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to enable or disable notifications for news and results of their favorite teams, with options to select notification types (e.g., goals, news, match start) and set notification frequency.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via Facebook, Twitter, and WhatsApp.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline access to previously loaded news, results, and team/player information, with cached content updated every 24 hours and expired after 48 hours.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall support Android (version 10 and above) and iOS (version 13 and above) devices.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High
