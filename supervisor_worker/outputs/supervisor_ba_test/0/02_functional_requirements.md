# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide users with current football news, live scores, and detailed team/player information for all national and international leagues and competitions.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and manage their favorite teams and sports channels for personalized content display.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide real-time notifications to users about news and live results for their selected favorite teams.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links from external providers and display them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and match reports directly to social media platforms.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live match data in real time via an external API.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup with an uncomplicated process.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to configure app modules and connect their own interface to the user interface for a personalized experience.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** Medium
