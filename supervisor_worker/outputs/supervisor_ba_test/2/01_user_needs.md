# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access football news, live scores, and team/player information quickly and reliably so that I can stay informed about my favorite teams and competitions.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app to check latest news, live scores, and team/player stats for their favorite teams and leagues.

### UN-002: I want to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels so that I only see relevant news and live updates.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers/logs in, selects favorite teams and channels, and receives tailored content and notifications.

### UN-003: I want to receive real-time notifications about news and live results for my favorite teams, with control over which types of notifications I receive.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications, selects preferred notification types, and receives push alerts for news and live events related to selected teams.

### UN-004: I want to watch live streams of football matches directly from the app when available and permitted in my country, using a seamless integration method.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User browses live matches, sees available streams, and accesses them via deep link or embedded player if transmission rights are fulfilled.

### UN-005: I want to share news and match reports with friends via supported social media platforms directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads a news article or match report and shares it to Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp, or Instagram.

### UN-006: I need the app to load and be ready to use within two seconds so that I can access information without delay.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User launches the app and expects immediate access to main features.

### UN-007: I need the app to function reliably even with limited network coverage and remain usable offline if the connection is lost, with clear information on which features are available offline.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses the app in areas with poor or no network and expects core features (news, scores, team info) to remain available based on last cached data.

### UN-008: I need my personal data to be processed securely and in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers, logs in, and uses the app, expecting privacy and data protection.

### UN-009: I need the app to support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance loss, especially during high-traffic events.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** On match days, many users access the app at the same time and expect consistent performance.

### UN-010: I need to detect and resolve technical malfunctions early so that the app remains stable and reliable.
- **User Group Type:** Secondary
- **User Group:** App Support Team
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** Support team monitors app health, tests before release, and evaluates user feedback for improvements.

### UN-011: I want to personalize the app interface by configuring modules, themes, and widgets, but not by integrating external APIs.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses settings to add/remove modules, select themes, and arrange widgets for a personalized interface.
