# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds of app launch.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably with limited network coverage and provide offline access to previously loaded content.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance degradation.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall comply with GDPR for all processing of personal user data.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available for both Android and iOS platforms with equivalent functionality.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes before release, and user feedback shall be continuously evaluated for improvements.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High
