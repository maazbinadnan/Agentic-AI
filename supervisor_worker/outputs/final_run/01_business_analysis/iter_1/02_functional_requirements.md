# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup using a simple and streamlined process, supporting registration and login via email/password, Google, Apple, and Facebook social login methods.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to select and manage their favorite teams and preferred sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news, filtered according to the user's selected favorite teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed team and player information accessible at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for football matches, displaying them only when the broadcast has begun and transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country. The system shall enforce geo-restriction by checking the user's location and only displaying live stream links when permitted. Live streams shall open in an external browser or native player, not within the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share football news and match reports via social media platforms directly from the app, supporting at minimum Facebook, Twitter, and WhatsApp, using the native OS sharing dialog.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a notification system that sends real-time alerts about news and live results for the user's favorite teams when notifications are enabled. The system shall allow users to select notification preferences by type, including goals, match start/end, news, and general updates.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain consistent user interface and module integration, regardless of external server updates.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to configure and connect additional modules to the user interface for personalized experiences. Only officially supported modules provided by the app developer shall be allowed; third-party integrations are not supported in the initial release.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** Medium
