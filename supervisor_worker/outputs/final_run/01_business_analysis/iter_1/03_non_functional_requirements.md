# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds of app launch.
- **Source:** UN-006
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably with limited network coverage and provide offline access to previously loaded news articles, team and player information, and user preferences. Live ticker and live stream features shall require an active internet connection. The system shall cache news, team, and player data for offline use and refresh this data automatically when connectivity is restored.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 simultaneous users without performance degradation.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall comply with GDPR for all processing of personal user data.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available for both Android and iOS platforms with equivalent functionality.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes before release, and user feedback shall be continuously evaluated for improvements.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High
