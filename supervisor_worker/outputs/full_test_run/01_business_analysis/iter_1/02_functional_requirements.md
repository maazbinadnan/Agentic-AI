# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite football teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to access detailed team and player information at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide live stream links for matches, only displaying them when transmission rights are fulfilled for the user's country, determined via a geo-IP or country-rights API.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter/X, WhatsApp, Instagram) directly from the app using native app sharing and deep linking.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide push notifications to users about news and results for their selected favorite teams when notifications are enabled, allowing users to select notification types (news, results, live events) and set quiet hours.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup using email/password, Google, Apple, and Facebook authentication, enforcing password complexity and providing account recovery options.
- **Source Need:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall support both Android and iOS platforms with consistent functionality.
- **Source Need:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to manage their GDPR consent, request data deletion, and export their personal data from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High
