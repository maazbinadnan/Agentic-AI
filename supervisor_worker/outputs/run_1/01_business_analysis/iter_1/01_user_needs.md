# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access current football news and live results for my favorite teams so that I can stay informed at all times.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app, selects favorite teams, and views news and live results on the dashboard.

### UN-002: I need to personalize my app experience by selecting and updating favorite teams and sports channels at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User configures and updates preferences during onboarding or in settings to tailor content.

### UN-003: I need to receive real-time notifications about news and results for my favorite teams, with control over notification types.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications and customizes which types of alerts to receive for relevant events.

### UN-004: I need to view live streams of football matches when available and permitted in my country.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses live stream links in the app when a broadcast is available and rights are fulfilled.

### UN-005: I need to access detailed team and player information at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User navigates to team or player profiles for in-depth statistics and info.

### UN-006: I need to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads an article or report and shares it using integrated social sharing options.

### UN-007: I need the app to load quickly and run smoothly, even under high load or poor network conditions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User launches the app and expects it to be ready for use within 2 seconds, regardless of device or network.

### UN-008: I need the app to function reliably and remain usable even if the network connection is temporarily lost, with clear indication of offline features.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User continues to use the app and access cached content during intermittent connectivity.

### UN-009: I need my personal data to be protected, processed in compliance with GDPR, and be able to manage or delete my data.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers, logs in, and uses the app with confidence that their data is secure, can be managed, and privacy is respected.

### UN-010: I need to register and log in quickly and easily at app startup, with support for multiple authentication methods.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User completes a simple registration or login process upon first launch, choosing from available authentication options.

### UN-011: I need the app to be available on both Android and iOS devices.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads and installs the app from the respective app store.

### UN-012: I need the app to be thoroughly tested and supported to avoid malfunctions and ensure ongoing reliability.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User expects a stable app experience due to comprehensive testing and ongoing support.
