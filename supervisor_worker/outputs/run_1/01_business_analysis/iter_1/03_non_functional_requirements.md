# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall fully load and be ready for user interaction within 2 seconds of app launch on supported devices.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance degradation.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably and provide access to cached content when the network connection is temporarily lost.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall process all personal data in compliance with GDPR requirements, including data retention and user deletion.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available for both Android and iOS platforms.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo comprehensive testing and support processes to detect and resolve malfunctions prior to release and throughout the app lifecycle.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High
