# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select, update, and remove their favorite football teams and sports channels at any time, with a maximum of 10 teams and 5 channels per user.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to access detailed team and player information at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for football matches, displaying them only when the broadcast is available and permitted in the user's country, determined by IP geolocation.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a notification system that sends real-time alerts about news and results for the user's favorite teams when notifications are enabled, allowing users to select notification types (e.g., goals, news, match start/end).
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a registration and login process that can be completed directly at app startup, supporting email/password and social login (Google, Apple, Facebook).
- **Source Need:** UN-010
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall ensure that the user interface remains consistent and functional after external server updates.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall ensure that all app modules remain functional after external server updates.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to access cached news, results, and team/player information offline, with clear indication of offline status and last update time.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to request deletion of their account and all associated personal data in compliance with GDPR.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High
