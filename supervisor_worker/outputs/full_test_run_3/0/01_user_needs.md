# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access current football news and live results for my favorite teams so that I can stay informed at all times.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app, selects favorite teams, and views news and live results on the dashboard.

### UN-002: I want to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers/logs in, configures favorite teams and channels, and receives tailored content.

### UN-003: I want to receive real-time notifications about news and results for my favorite teams.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications and receives push alerts for relevant events.

### UN-004: I want to view live stream links for matches when available and permitted in my country.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User browses match details and accesses live stream links if rights are fulfilled.

### UN-005: I want to access detailed team and player information at any time.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User searches or navigates to team/player profiles for in-depth information.

### UN-006: I want to share news and game reports via social media directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads an article or report and shares it using integrated social sharing options.

### UN-007: I need the app to load quickly and run smoothly, even with high user traffic or poor network conditions.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User launches the app and expects it to be ready for use within 2 seconds, regardless of network or load.

### UN-008: I need the app to function reliably and remain usable even if the network connection is temporarily lost.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User continues to use the app during intermittent connectivity without crashes or data loss.

### UN-009: I want my personal data to be protected and processed in compliance with GDPR.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers, logs in, and uses the app with confidence that their data is secure and privacy is respected.

### UN-010: I want the app to be available on both Android and iOS devices.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads and installs the app from the respective app store for their device.
