# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to register and log in directly at app startup using a simple authentication process.
- **Source Need:** UN-002, UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to select and manage their favorite teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to access detailed team and player information at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for matches, displaying them only when the broadcast rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a notification system that sends real-time alerts about news and results for the user's favorite teams when notifications are enabled.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide consistent access to all modules and the user interface, regardless of external server updates.
- **Source Need:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High
