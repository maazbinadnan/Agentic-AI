# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall be fully loaded and ready for user interaction within a maximum of 2 seconds after app start under normal operating conditions on supported Android and iOS devices.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall support at least 100,000 concurrent users on match days without degradation of core application performance for news access, live ticker updates, and navigation.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall maintain reliable operation under limited network coverage and shall recover gracefully from temporary connection loss without application crash or data corruption.
- **Source:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall process personal data in compliance with GDPR requirements applicable to user registration, login, preferences, notifications, and related personal data handling.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall keep the core user interface available and navigable during external server data updates, including continued access to primary navigation and locally rendered screens without requiring an app restart.
- **Source:** UN-001
- **Priority:** Medium
