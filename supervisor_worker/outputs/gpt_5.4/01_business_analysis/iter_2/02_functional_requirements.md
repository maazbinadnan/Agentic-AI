# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a unified mobile user interface for Android and iOS that allows users to access football news, extensive coverage of leagues and competitions across national and international football, and detailed team and player information from within the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and manage favorite teams and preferred sports channels, and shall use these preferences to personalize displayed news and live result content.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves live match data from an external API and displays updated live results and match events to users in real time as updated data is made available by the external API.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall display live stream links for matches only after the associated live broadcast has begun and only when transmission rights are valid in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide registration and login capabilities directly at app startup to enable users to access personalized features.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate with a notification service and shall send notifications about news or results related to users' favorite teams when the notification function is activated.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news items and match reports from within the app through social media sharing options supported by the device.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall remain usable when network connectivity is temporarily unavailable by allowing access to the app interface, locally stored user configuration options, and content that was previously loaded on the device, and shall clearly indicate when features requiring live server data are unavailable during offline conditions.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High
