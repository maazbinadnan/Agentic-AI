# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite football teams and sports channels, and shall store these preferences securely in the cloud to enable synchronization across devices.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels, and shall cache the latest news articles for offline access.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time, and shall display the most recent results available when offline.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to receive push notifications about news and results for their favorite teams, and shall allow users to configure notification preferences by type (e.g., news, results, all events).
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to live stream links for matches, displaying them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country, and shall update and manage live stream provider links dynamically via a secure, rights-aware API.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to view detailed information about teams and players, and shall cache recently viewed team and player profiles for offline access.
- **Source Need:** UN-005, UN-009
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms including Facebook, Twitter, and WhatsApp directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a registration and login process that can be completed directly at app startup with minimal steps, supporting both email/password and third-party authentication (Google, Apple, Facebook).
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that integrates all modules and allows direct access to news, live ticker, streams, and team/player information.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High
