# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds after app startup.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably and provide access to previously loaded news articles, live results, and team/player information when offline or with limited network coverage. Cached data shall be refreshed automatically when connectivity is restored, at least once every 24 hours.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall be capable of supporting up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance degradation.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available for both Android and iOS platforms.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall process and store all personal data in compliance with GDPR, and shall provide users with the ability to request data export, deletion, and rectification directly from the app.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes before release to detect and resolve malfunctions.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously evaluate user feedback and app reviews to improve functionality.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Medium
