# 1. High-Level User Needs

### UN-001: I need to access current football news and live results for my favorite teams so that I can stay informed at all times.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User opens the app, selects favorite teams, and views news and live results on the dashboard.

### UN-002: I need to personalize my app experience by selecting favorite teams and sports channels, and have my preferences available across devices.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User configures preferences during onboarding or in settings to tailor content, expecting preferences to be saved and synced.

### UN-003: I need to receive real-time notifications about news and results for my favorite teams, with control over which types of notifications I receive.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User enables notifications and selects preferred notification types for relevant events.

### UN-004: I need to access live stream links for matches when available and permitted in my country, and know that these links are up-to-date.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User browses match details and clicks on live stream links when rights are available and links are current.

### UN-005: I need to view detailed team and player information at any time, including when offline.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User searches or navigates to team/player profiles for in-depth stats and info, expecting access even with limited connectivity.

### UN-006: I need to share news and game reports via my preferred social media platforms directly from the app.
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** Medium
- **User Journey Context:** User reads an article or report and uses the share function to post on supported social media.

### UN-007: I need to register and log in quickly and easily at app startup, using my preferred method (email/password or social login).
- **User Group Type:** Primary
- **User Group:** Football Fan
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads the app, registers or logs in with minimal steps using email/password or third-party authentication, and accesses main features.

### UN-008: I need the app to load and be ready to use within two seconds after starting.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User launches the app and expects immediate access to content.

### UN-009: I need the app to function reliably even with limited network coverage and remain usable offline, with access to key features and recently viewed data.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses the app in areas with poor connectivity and expects continued usability and access to cached content.

### UN-010: I need assurance that my personal data is protected and processed in compliance with GDPR, including the ability to manage my data rights.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User registers and uses the app, expecting privacy, data protection, and the ability to request data export, deletion, or correction.

### UN-011: I need the app to remain stable and performant even during high traffic, such as match days.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User accesses the app during peak times and expects no performance degradation.

### UN-012: I need the app to be available on both Android and iOS devices.
- **User Group Type:** Overarching
- **User Group:** All Users
- **Demand Level:** High
- **User Journey Context:** User downloads and installs the app from the respective app store.
