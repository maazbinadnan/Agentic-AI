# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite football teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall enable users to receive push notifications about news and results for their favorite teams when notifications are enabled.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide access to live stream links for matches, displaying them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to view detailed information about teams and players.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a registration and login process that can be completed directly at app startup with minimal steps.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a user interface that integrates all modules and allows direct access to news, live ticker, streams, and team/player information.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High
