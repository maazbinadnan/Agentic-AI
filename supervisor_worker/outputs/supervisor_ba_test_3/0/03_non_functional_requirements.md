# 3. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall load and be fully ready for user interaction within two seconds of app launch.
- **Source:** UN-008
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without performance degradation.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall function reliably with limited network coverage and provide offline access to previously loaded content.
- **Source:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall process and store all personal data in compliance with GDPR.
- **Source:** UN-010
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall be available for both Android and iOS platforms.
- **Source:** UN-012
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall undergo thorough testing and support processes before release to detect and resolve malfunctions.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** High

### NFR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall continuously evaluate user feedback and app reviews to improve functionality.
- **Source:** UN-011
- **Priority:** Medium
