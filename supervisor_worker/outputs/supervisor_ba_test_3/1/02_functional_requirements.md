# 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-001
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to select and follow their favorite football teams and sports channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-002
- **Requirement:** The system shall display current football news relevant to the user's selected favorite teams and channels.
- **Source Need:** UN-001, UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-003
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a live ticker module that retrieves and displays live results for the user's favorite teams in real time.
- **Source Need:** UN-001
- **Priority:** High

### FR-004
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide detailed team and player information accessible at any time.
- **Source Need:** UN-005
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-005
- **Requirement:** The system shall integrate live stream links for matches, displaying them only when transmission rights are fulfilled in the user's country.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-006
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to share news articles and game reports via social media platforms directly from the app.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-007
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide a notification system that sends real-time alerts about news and results for the user's favorite teams when notifications are enabled.
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-008
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide registration and login functionality that can be completed directly at app startup with minimal steps.
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High

### FR-009
- **Requirement:** The system shall ensure that all modules and the user interface remain consistent and operational even when external server updates occur.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-010
- **Requirement:** The system shall provide offline access to previously loaded news, live ticker data, and team/player information, and shall cache this data for offline use.
- **Source Need:** UN-009
- **Priority:** High

### FR-011
- **Requirement:** The system shall store user personalization data (favorite teams, channels, notification preferences) securely in the cloud and locally, and shall synchronize preferences across devices when the user is logged in.
- **Source Need:** UN-002
- **Priority:** High

### FR-012
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow users to customize notification preferences by event type (e.g., goals, news, match start/end, all events).
- **Source Need:** UN-003
- **Priority:** High

### FR-013
- **Requirement:** The system shall support sharing to at least Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp, and Instagram, and shall use native app integration or deep linking where available.
- **Source Need:** UN-006
- **Priority:** Medium

### FR-014
- **Requirement:** The system shall determine user location and verify streaming rights for live content before displaying live stream links.
- **Source Need:** UN-004
- **Priority:** High

### FR-015
- **Requirement:** The system shall support registration and login via email/password and third-party authentication providers (Google, Apple, Facebook).
- **Source Need:** UN-007
- **Priority:** High
