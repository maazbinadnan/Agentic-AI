No.	Functional Requirements
1	The app must allow users to individually select and display current football news of their favorite team from preferred sports channels.
2	The app must allow users to view live results of their favorite teams in real time via a live ticker.
3	The app must allow users to view league tables and statistics from current as well as past seasons.
4	The app must provide users with detailed team and player information that can be accessed at any time.
5	As soon as a live match begins, the app must allow users to access live stream links from providers such as DAZN or Sky, as long as the broadcast rights are valid.
6	The app must allow users to register easily and log in directly from the start page.
7	The app must allow users to personalize their experience by following their favorite teams and leagues.
8	The app must allow users to select favorite teams and national teams to receive push notifications about news and updates.
9	The app must allow users to receive push notifications about news or results of their favorite teams.
10	The app must allow users to share news and match reports directly via social media.
11	The live ticker module must allow users to retrieve and display live data.
12	The app must allow users to access live match results through the user interface directly.
13	The user interface must allow users to view and manage content and modules together.
14	The registration system must allow the registration process to be carried out quickly and easily.
15	The notification system must allow the app to send users information about current events once activated.
