# Personalized Society Recommendations

As a first-year undergraduate student, I want to receive personalized recommendations for societies based on my interests and course, so that I can easily find groups that align with my niche hobbies without feeling overwhelmed during the freshers' fair.

**Acceptance Criteria:**  
1. **If** I have filled out my profile with interests and course information,  
**Then** the university app should analyze this data to suggest 3 or 4 societies,  
**When** I access the recommendations feature on the app.
